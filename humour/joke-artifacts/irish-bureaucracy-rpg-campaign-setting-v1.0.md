# 🎲 IRISH BUREAUCRACY: THE CAMPAIGN SETTING

A surrealist administrative survival RPG set in the Republic of Paperwork

---

I. SETTING OVERVIEW

Welcome to The Republic of Paperwork, a sprawling bureaucratic realm where every agency is a dungeon, every form is a trap, and every official email is a cryptic spell scroll.

The land is ruled not by kings, but by Departments, Statutory Bodies, and Units Whose Remit Is Unclear.

Magic exists, but only in the form of:

- procedural recursion,  
- contradictory correspondence,  
- and the mysterious power of “the appropriate unit.”

The player characters are Petitioners, wandering the administrative landscape seeking:

- rectification,  
- accommodation,  
- access to justice,  
- and occasionally a book renewal.

---

IV. CORE MECHANICS

1. The Remit System
Every agency has a Remit, which is:

- undefined,  
- contradictory,  
- and changes mid‑combat.

Players must roll Insight to determine whether the agency can legally act.  
It always fails.

---

2. The Accommodation Check
When requesting accommodation, roll:

- Disability (d20)  
- Trauma (d20)  
- Bureaucracy (d20)

If all three dice match, the Accessibility Officer appears.  
This has never happened.

---

3. The Timeline Paradox
Time does not behave normally.

- 30 days = 2 weeks  
- 2 weeks = “you didn’t wait long enough”  
- 1 month = “we are experiencing a high volume of correspondence”  

Players must track time using the Calendar of Confusion, which has 17 months.

---

V. CAMPAIGN ARC

Act I: The Exclusion Letter Prophecy
The party receives a mysterious letter banning them from a campus they never entered.

Act II: The Article 16 Pilgrimage
They seek rectification from the DPC, entering the Slime Tunnel.

Act III: The FOI Identity Crisis
They fall into the HSE Foam Pit and must prove their own existence.

Act IV: The WRC Time‑Dilation Trials
They navigate the maze of deadlines that contradict physics.

Act V: The Final Boss — The Appropriate Unit
If the party survives, they face the mythical Appropriate Unit.

It has no stats.  
It cannot be defeated.  
It cannot be found.  
It simply is.

---

VI. CAMPAIGN REWARD

If the party completes the campaign, they receive:

- A renewed library book,  
- A corrected name,  
- A single functioning public service,  
- And 1d4 emotional stability points.
