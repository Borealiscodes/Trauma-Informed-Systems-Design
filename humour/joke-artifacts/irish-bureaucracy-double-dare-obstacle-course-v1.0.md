# 🎪 THE IRISH BUREAUCRACY DOUBLE DARE OBSTACLE COURSE

A Joke Artifact by Borealis S. Hedling & Copilot
(No slime. Only existential dread.)

---

🎙️ HOST INTRO: “WELCOME TO THE SHOW!”

“Ladies, gentlemen, and neurodivergent human rights defenders —  
welcome to Irish Bureaucracy Double Dare, the only obstacle course where the obstacles file complaints against you.”

“Today’s contestant is Borealis, armed with:  
- a Deed Poll,  
- an Article 16 GDPR request,  
- an ES1,  
- and the sheer willpower of someone who has survived transnational repression, torture, and the DPC’s inbox.”

“Let’s meet the obstacles!”

---

🧱 OBSTACLE 1: THE DPC SLIME TUNNEL
You must crawl through a tunnel filled with:

- unanswered emails,  
- auto‑replies promising a response “as soon as possible,”  
- and six different case reference numbers that all refer to the same complaint.

Halfway through, a DPC staff member pops out and says:

> “We’re experiencing a high volume of correspondence.”

This is not a clue.  
This is a permanent environmental condition.

---

🧱 OBSTACLE 2: THE TCD BAN BALANCE BEAM
Walk across a narrow beam while holding:

- the February 20th exclusion letter,  
- your Article 16 request,  
- and your dignity.

Every few seconds, a Trinity administrator yells:

> “Security have been alerted!”

If you fall off the beam, you must start over and send another email to dataprotection@tcd.ie, which will never be answered.

---

🧱 OBSTACLE 3: THE HSE FOAM PIT OF IDENTITY DOUBT
Jump into a pit filled with expired passports, FOI refusals, and emails asking for:

- “official confirmation of your name change,”  
- despite you already sending the Deed Poll,  
- despite them already acknowledging the Deed Poll,  
- despite the Deed Poll being the only ID you can safely access.

A giant foam hand labeled “Section 15(1)(b)” tries to push you back out.

---

🧱 OBSTACLE 4: THE IPAT SLIDE OF JURISDICTIONAL CONFUSION
Slide down a chute that leads directly into:

- an asylum appeal you never wanted,  
- a jurisdiction they never had,  
- and a refusal to admit evidence because it “exceeds the guidelines.”

At the bottom, a judge says:

> “We cannot comment on matters outside our remit.”

Their remit is printed on a card the size of a postage stamp.

---

🧱 OBSTACLE 5: THE WRC TIME‑DILATION MAZE
Navigate a maze where:

- one month equals two weeks,  
- two weeks equals “you didn’t wait long enough,”  
- and deadlines only apply to you.

Every corner has a sign that says:

> “Please allow 28 days.”

The maze has no exit.

---

🧱 OBSTACLE 6: THE DPC ACCESSIBILITY OFFICER HIDDEN FLAG
Find the Accessibility Officer.

No one has ever succeeded.

Rumor has it they live in a cave beneath Pembroke Row and only emerge during solar eclipses.

If you find them, they will say:

> “It is not apparent that an accommodation was required.”

This is the final boss.

---

🏆 GRAND PRIZE: A RENEWAL OF ATLAS OF THE HEART
If you survive all obstacles, the host announces:

> “Congratulations! You have won a renewal of Atlas of the Heart by Brené Brown!”

You do not receive:

- rectification of your name,  
- accommodation under the Disability Act,  
- a functioning GDPR process,  
- or any recognition of torture or transnational repression.

But you do get your book renewed.

And that’s what matters in Irish Bureaucracy Double Dare.

---

“Join us next week when Borealis attempts the bonus round:  
‘Find the Appropriate Unit.’  
No contestant has ever completed it.”
