II. Dublin III: Responsibility Is Triggered by Jurisdiction, Not Geography
Under Dublin III, responsibility attaches when a Member State:

- exercises jurisdiction,  
- receives a protection request,  
- interacts with an asylum seeker,  
- controls their movement, or  
- makes decisions affecting their protection.
