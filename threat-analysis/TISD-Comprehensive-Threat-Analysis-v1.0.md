4. Regulatory Threat: Oversight Bodies Ignoring or Signposting Without Warm Transfer

4.1. Ignored (HIQA, IHREC, civil society)
Being ignored is a direct structural threat.

4.2. Signposted (NDA)
Signposting without warm transfer is ethically unacceptable.

4.3. Warm Transfer Standard

A warm transfer requires:

- direct contact with the next body,  
- explanation of the case,  
- confirmation of accommodations,  
- assurance of non‑retaliation,  
- confirmation of follow‑through.

Without warm transfer, signposting is abandonment.
