III. Mandate‑by‑Mandate Impact Analysis

A. Special Rapporteur on Torture (UNCAT Article 14 & GC3)
Relevance:  
Your update centers on Ireland’s refusal to provide:

- rehabilitation,  
- medical care,  
- legal access,  
- disability accommodations,  
- trauma‑informed procedures,  
- and safe housing.

These are core Article 14 obligations.

GC3 states:

- redress must be “adequate, effective, and comprehensive” (GC3 ¶6),  
- rehabilitation must include medical, psychological, legal, and social services (GC3 ¶11),  
- services must be provided “as soon as possible” (GC3 ¶15),  
- access cannot depend on formal judicial processes (GC3 ¶15),  
- victims must be able to obtain redress, not merely seek it (GC3 ¶20).

Your update shows Ireland has violated every single one of these requirements.

Impact:  
This mandates immediate inquiry and potential communication to Ireland.
