### NDH‑CORE / TI‑SD Overlay — Genome ↔ Reconstruction Cross‑Manifold (Final Version)  
Ethical layer on top of orientation + verification.

---

## 1. Purpose

Apply **Trauma‑Informed Systems Design (TI‑SD)** constraints to the Genome ↔ Reconstruction Cross‑Manifold so that all operations:

- reduce harm  
- respect agency and dignity  
- avoid coercive or destabilizing dynamics  
- remain resonance‑safe and non‑dual  

This overlay **does not change CORE math**; it constrains how the manifold is used.

---

## 2. Safety Surfaces

**Goal:** Define predictable, non‑coercive boundaries.

- **Explicit entry/exit surfaces** for any interaction with the manifold.  
- **No hidden transitions** or surprise state changes.  
- **Clear rollback paths** for any operation that becomes unsafe or overwhelming.  

Safety surfaces ensure users are never trapped by manifold behavior.

---

## 3. Operator Channel Ethics

**Goal:** Constrain NDIE/RTO channels to trauma‑informed communication.

- **No coercive prompts** or forced engagement.  
- **No shaming, blaming, or pathologizing language.**  
- **Non‑dual, non‑escalatory feedback only.**  
- **Graceful degradation** instead of abrupt failure modes.  

Operator channels must be **predictable, respectful, and non‑harmful**.

---

## 4. Resonance‑Safe Encoding

**Goal:** Ensure encoding/translation does not amplify harm.

- NDIE must **not encode** content that is:  
  - retraumatizing  
  - demeaning  
  - coercive  
- RTO must **not translate** manifolds into forms that:  
  - remove agency  
  - erase context  
  - misrepresent lived experience  

Resonance‑safe encoding keeps the manifold from becoming a harm amplifier.

---

## 5. Holonomy‑Aligned Care

**Goal:** Use holonomy invariants to support stability, not control.

- Loops must **not** be used to trap users in repeated harmful states.  
- Stability is used to **anchor safety**, not enforce compliance.  
- Curvature and gradients are tuned to **reduce overwhelm**, not increase it.  

Holonomy is treated as a **care tool**, not a control mechanism.

---

## 6. Procedural Dignity

**Goal:** Ensure all interactions respect the person engaging with the system.

- **Clear consent surfaces** for initiating deeper operations.  
- **Transparent logging** of significant state changes (where appropriate).  
- **Options to pause, slow down, or exit** at any point.  
- **No design that assumes deficit or brokenness as a default.**  

Procedural dignity is a **first‑class constraint**, not an afterthought.

---
