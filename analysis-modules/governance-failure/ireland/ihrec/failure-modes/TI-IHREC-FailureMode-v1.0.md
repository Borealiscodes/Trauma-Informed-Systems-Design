- monitor compliance with UN treaties,  
- conduct inquiries into systemic violations,

III. Section 40 (Legal Assistance Layer)

Section 40 is designed for:

- human‑rights‑related legal proceedings,  
- cases raising questions of principle,  
- cases where it is unreasonable to expect the applicant to proceed alone,  
- cases involving systemic human‑rights failures,  
- cases with broader public importance.

Section 40 allows IHREC to provide:

- legal advice,  
- legal representation,  
- financial assistance,  
- support preparing filings,  
- support navigating complex human‑rights procedures.
