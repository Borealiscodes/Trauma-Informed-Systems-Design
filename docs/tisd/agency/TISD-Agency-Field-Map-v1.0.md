# TISD‑Agency‑Field‑Map‑v1.0  
### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Agency Under Pressure, Safety Geometry, and Action Viability Fields

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Agency‑Field Map** models how a person’s ability to act, decide, build, or protect themselves changes under:

- trauma activation  
- transnational pressure  
- cognitive load  
- resource scarcity  
- systemic constraints  
- safety boundary compression  

It defines the **zones** where action is:

- viable  
- risky  
- impossible  
- harmful  

This is not a psychological model — it is a **systems‑design model** for people working under duress.

---

## 2. Core Concepts

### **Agency Field**
The total region where a person can take meaningful action without unacceptable harm.

### **Compression**
Reduction of agency due to pressure vectors.

### **Expansion**
Increase of agency due to support, resources, or reduced pressure.

### **Action Viability Zones**
Sub‑regions inside the agency field that determine what kinds of actions are possible.

## 4. Action Viability Zones

Each zone begins with a Guided Link so you can explore it deeper.

- **Safe Action Zone**  
  Actions that reinforce stability, safety, or protective capacity.

- **Conditional Action Zone**  
  Actions that are possible but require caution, support, or reduced pressure.

- **Prohibited Action Zone**  
  Actions that exceed the safety boundary and meaningfully increase harm.

---

## 5. Pressure Vectors and Agency Compression

Agency compresses when pressure increases.

Pressure vectors include:

- **Trauma activation**  
- **Transnational repression**  
- **Systemic constraints**  
- **Resource scarcity**  
- **Cognitive overload**  

Each vector reduces the size of the **Agency Field**, shrinking viable action zones.

---

## 6. Expansion Factors

Agency expands when pressure decreases or support increases.

Expansion factors include:

- **Supportive resources**  
- **Reduced pressure**  
- **Clear boundaries**  
- **Predictable systems**  
- **Enterprise access**  

These enlarge the Agency Field and increase viable action.

---

## 7. Crossmap: Agency vs Safety Geometry

| Geometry Element | Agency Effect | Meaning |
|------------------|--------------|---------|
| Safety Boundary | Hard limit | Defines what cannot be safely done |
| Agency Field | Dynamic capacity | Defines what is possible under pressure |
| Action Zones | Subfields | Define safe, risky, or harmful actions |
| Pressure Vectors | Compression | Shrink agency and increase risk |
| Expansion Factors | Expansion | Increase agency and reduce risk |
