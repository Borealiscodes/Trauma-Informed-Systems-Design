### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Time‑Dependent Pressure Dynamics, Boundary Shifts, and Agency Oscillation

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Temporal‑Pressure Map** models how:

- pressure vectors  
- stabilization vectors  
- safety boundaries  
- agency fields  
- tool zones  

change **over time**, not just in static snapshots.

This artifact explains:

- why pressure spikes collapse agency  
- why stabilization sometimes creates only temporary openings  
- why boundaries shift unpredictably under duress  
- why NDH/TISD work viability fluctuates  
- why “capacity” is not a constant  

---

## 2. Core Concepts

### **Temporal Pressure**
Pressure that changes over time due to external events, internal state, or systemic behavior.

### **Temporal Stabilization**
Support or clarity that increases or decreases over time.

### **Boundary Drift**
Movement of the safety boundary as pressure or stabilization changes.

### **Agency Oscillation**
Expansion or contraction of agency fields over time.

### **Tool Zone Viability**
Whether NDH/TISD work is possible at a given moment.

## 4. Temporal Modes

### **Mode 1 — Pressure Spike**
Sudden increase in pressure.

Effects:

- boundary contracts rapidly  
- agency collapses  
- tool zone becomes non‑viable  
- cognitive overload likely  

### **Mode 2 — Stabilization Window**
Temporary increase in stabilization.

Effects:

- boundary expands  
- agency opens  
- tool zone becomes viable  
- NDH/TISD work possible  

### **Mode 3 — Oscillation**
Pressure and stabilization alternate.

Effects:

- boundary instability  
- agency volatility  
- unpredictable viability  
- requires careful pacing  

### **Mode 4 — Sustained Pressure**
Long-term pressure without relief.

Effects:

- boundary erosion  
- chronic agency compression  
- tool zone collapse  
- burnout risk  

### **Mode 5 — Sustained Stabilization**
Long-term stabilization.

Effects:

- boundary reinforcement  
- agency expansion  
- tool zone stability  
- sustainable NDH/TISD work
