## 4. Temporal Interaction Modes

### **Mode 1 — Pressure‑Dominant Temporal Curve**
Pressure increases faster than stabilization.

Effects:

- boundary contracts  
- agency collapses  
- tool zone becomes non‑viable  

### **Mode 2 — Stabilization‑Dominant Temporal Curve**
Stabilization increases faster than pressure.

Effects:

- boundary expands  
- agency opens  
- tool zone becomes viable  

### **Mode 3 — Temporal Collision**
Pressure rises while stabilization falls.

Effects:

- boundary destabilizes  
- agency shrinks  
- collapse risk increases  

### **Mode 4 — Temporal Windowing**
Stabilization rises while pressure falls.

Effects:

- boundary stabilizes  
- agency expands  
- tool zone becomes temporarily viable  

### **Mode 5 — Temporal Oscillation**
Pressure and stabilization alternate.

Effects:

- boundary volatility  
- agency instability  
- intermittent tool‑zone viability
