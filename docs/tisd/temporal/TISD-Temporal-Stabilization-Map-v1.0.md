## 1. Purpose

The **Temporal‑Stabilization Map** explains how stabilization forces:

- emerge  
- strengthen  
- weaken  
- collapse  
- recover  

over time, and how these temporal changes affect:

- safety boundaries  
- agency fields  
- tool zone viability  
- pressure resistance  

This artifact shows *why stabilization windows matter* and *why some openings are durable while others are fragile*.

---

## 2. Core Concepts

### **Temporal Stabilization**
Stabilization that changes over time due to internal recovery, external support, or systemic behavior.

### **Stabilization Window**
A temporary period where stabilization exceeds pressure.

### **Stabilization Arc**
The full temporal curve of stabilization: rise → peak → decay → recovery.

### **Boundary Reinforcement**
Strengthening of the safety boundary over time.

### **Agency Recovery**
Expansion of agency following stabilization.

## 4. Temporal Stabilization Modes

### **Mode 1 — Stabilization Rise**
Stabilization begins increasing.

Effects:

- boundary begins expanding  
- agency begins opening  
- tool zone becomes *approaching viable*  

### **Mode 2 — Stabilization Peak**
Stabilization reaches maximum strength.

Effects:

- boundary strongly reinforced  
- agency fully open  
- tool zone highly viable  
- NDH/TISD work sustainable  

### **Mode 3 — Stabilization Decay**
Stabilization begins decreasing.

Effects:

- boundary begins contracting  
- agency begins shrinking  
- tool zone viability becomes conditional  

### **Mode 4 — Stabilization Collapse**
Stabilization drops below pressure.

Effects:

- boundary destabilizes  
- agency collapses  
- tool zone becomes non‑viable  

### **Mode 5 — Stabilization Recovery**
Stabilization begins rising again after collapse.

Effects:

- boundary re‑stabilizes  
- agency re‑opens  
- tool zone becomes viable again

## 7. Interaction with Temporal Pressure

Stabilization interacts with pressure in **three temporal patterns**:

- **Counter‑pressure:** Stabilization rises as pressure rises.  
- **Windowing:** Stabilization rises while pressure falls.  
- **Collision:** Pressure rises while stabilization falls.

These patterns determine whether boundaries expand or collapse.
