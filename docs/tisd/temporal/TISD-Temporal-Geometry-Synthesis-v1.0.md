## **5. Temporal Geometry Modes**

### **Mode 1 — Pressure‑Dominant Geometry**
Pressure rises faster than stabilization.

- boundaries contract  
- agency collapses  
- tool zone fails  

### **Mode 2 — Stabilization‑Dominant Geometry**
Stabilization rises faster than pressure.

- boundaries expand  
- agency opens  
- tool zone viable  

### **Mode 3 — Temporal Collision Geometry**
Pressure ↑ while stabilization ↓.

- boundary destabilization  
- agency shrinkage  
- collapse risk  

### **Mode 4 — Window Geometry**
Stabilization ↑ while pressure ↓.

- boundary reinforcement  
- agency expansion  
- temporary viability  

### **Mode 5 — Oscillation Geometry**
Pressure and stabilization alternate.

- boundary volatility  
- agency instability  
- intermittent viability
