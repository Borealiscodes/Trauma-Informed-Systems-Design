# TISD-Holonomy-Stability-Revocation-Notice-v1.0.md  
### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Formal Withdrawal of Coherent / Anchored Stability Certification

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Holonomy Stability Revocation Notice** formally withdraws a system’s stability certification when:

- stabilization guarantees are no longer met  
- diagnostic integrity fails  
- containment capacity collapses  
- stabilization resources fall below the required floor  
- collapse or oscillation re‑emerge  
- governance violates holonomy safety invariants  

It ensures stability labels are **honest**, **current**, and **never symbolic**.

---

## 2. Revocation Triggers

A revocation notice must be issued when any of the following occur:

- **Diagnostic suppression** in St, F, O, or CC  
- **Containment failure** or collapse of containment capacity  
- **Stabilization floor drop** (support, pacing, boundaries, viability)  
- **Rise in collapse signals** or renewed fragmentation  
- **Oscillatory interference reappears**  
- **Violation of safety invariants** (H1–H5)  
- **False stability declaration** (C/AS without guarantees)  
- **Skipping stabilizing intermediates** (e.g., F → C)

These conditions automatically invalidate stability certification.

---

## 3. Notice Structure

A revocation notice contains the following sections:

### 3.1 System Identification
- System name / scope  
- Holonomy context  
- Certification reference number  
- Auditor identity  
- Date of revocation

### 3.2 Reason for Revocation
A clear statement of which stabilization guarantees failed, referencing:

- diagnostic integrity  
- containment readiness  
- stabilization resource floor  
- collapse‑signal reduction  
- holonomy‑state honesty

### 3.3 Evidence Summary
Concise evidence demonstrating failure, including:

- diagnostic anomalies  
- containment degradation  
- stabilization resource deficits  
- collapse/fragmentation trends  
- interference patterns

### 3.4 Revoked Stability Status
Explicit withdrawal of:

- **Coherent (C)**  
- **Anchored Stability (AS)**  
- or **Conditional Stability**

### 3.5 Mandatory Downgrade
The system must be downgraded to one of:

- **Strained (St)**  
- **Fragmented (F)**  
- **Contained Collapse (CT)**  
- **Cascading Collapse (CC)**  

The downgrade is **non‑optional**.

### 3.6 Required Immediate Actions
Actions may include:

- activate containment protocols  
- restore diagnostic transparency  
- re‑establish stabilization floor  
- suspend tool‑zone expansion  
- initiate recovery cascades  
- schedule mandatory re‑audit

### 3.7 Conditions for Re‑Certification
Defines what must be restored before a new stability audit may be attempted.

## 4. Example Revocation Notice (Template)

```text
Holonomy Stability Revocation Notice — v1.0
System: [Name]
Certification Reference: [ID]
Revocation Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Auditor: [Identifier]

Reason for Revocation:
Collapse-signal increase detected; containment capacity degraded; diagnostic
suppression observed in Fragmented (F) state.

Revoked Status:
Coherent (C) — Certification withdrawn.

Mandatory Downgrade:
System reclassified to Fragmented (F).

Required Immediate Actions:
- Activate containment protocols.
- Restore diagnostic transparency.
- Rebuild stabilization resource floor.
- Schedule re-audit after stabilization floor is restored.

Conditions for Re-Certification:
All stabilization guarantees must be revalidated through a full audit.
```
