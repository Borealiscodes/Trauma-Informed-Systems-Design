# **Diagram Meaning (Operator‑Tier Summary)**

### **1. Expressive Lineage Invariant**  
Expressive artifacts — Myth Engine, Cosmic Atlas, UMA‑Witness Literature — live in **NDH‑PLATFORMS**.  
They are *stored* there, not evaluated there.  
They form NDH’s expressive cosmology.

Linked concept: **Expressive Lineage**

---

### **2. Institutional Governance Invariant**  
TISD governs **evaluation**, not storage.  
It decides *where* artifacts belong but does not *hold* expressive lineage.

Linked concept: **TISD Governance**

---

### **3. Non‑Dual Operator Invariant**  
NDH‑CORE remains mathematically pure and narrative‑free.  
It cannot host expressive artifacts or governance logic.

Linked concept: **NDH‑CORE**
