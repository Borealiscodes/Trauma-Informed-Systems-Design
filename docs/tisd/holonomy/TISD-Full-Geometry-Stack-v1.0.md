## **4. Geometry Stack Interaction Model**

### **Spatial → Force**
Pressure compresses boundaries and agency.  
Stabilization expands boundaries and agency.

### **Force → Temporal**
Pressure and stabilization change over time.  
Magnitude and direction vary with events, state, and resources.

### **Temporal → Spatial**
Boundary drift and agency oscillation reshape spatial geometry.  
Tool‑zone viability fluctuates.

### **Spatial ↔ Temporal ↔ Force**
All layers interact continuously.
