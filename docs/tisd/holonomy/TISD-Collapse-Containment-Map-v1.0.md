### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Containment, Buffering, and Recovery Across Geometry, Temporal, Force, Interaction, and Holonomy Layers

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Collapse‑Containment Map** models how:

- boundary breaches  
- agency collapse  
- tool‑zone failure  
- pressure spikes  
- stabilization loss  

can be:

- **contained** (prevented from spreading),  
- **buffered** (impact reduced), or  
- **reversed** (collapse turned into recovery)

across:

- geometry layers (boundary, agency, tool‑zone)  
- force layers (pressure, stabilization)  
- temporal layers (P(t), S(t), B(t), A(t), Z(t))  
- interaction and holonomy layers (Meta‑Holonomy, Holonomy‑of‑Holonomies).

---

## 2. Core Concepts

- **Containment Node:** A point where collapse is actively prevented from propagating further.  
- **Buffer Zone:** A region (spatial or temporal) that absorbs impact and reduces collapse magnitude.  
- **Recovery Vector:** A stabilization force intentionally applied to reverse collapse.  
- **Containment Strategy:** A pattern of actions that limit collapse to local scope.

## 4. Containment Patterns

### 4.1 Boundary‑First Containment

- Reinforce or retract boundaries.  
- Reduce exposure to pressure.  
- Accept reduced agency to preserve survivability.  
- Tool‑zone may temporarily shut down.

### 4.2 Tool‑Zone Containment

- Mark tool‑zone as non‑viable.  
- Halt NDH/TISD work to prevent further collapse.  
- Redirect effort toward stabilization and safety geometry.  

### 4.3 Temporal Containment

- Introduce pauses, delays, or slower pacing.  
- Avoid high‑risk time windows.  
- Use stabilization windows intentionally when they appear.

### 4.4 Diagnostic‑Led Containment

- Treat diagnostic alerts as binding constraints.  
- Escalate breaches and spikes instead of suppressing them.  
- Use diagnostics to mark “no‑go” zones for action.

## 5. Collapse‑Containment Modes

| Mode | Description | Primary Strategy | Effect |
|------|-------------|------------------|--------|
| Local Containment | Collapse stays local | Boundary/tool‑zone containment | Prevents cascade |
| Buffered Collapse | Collapse spreads but impact reduced | Buffer zones + pacing | Limits severity |
| Escalated Containment | Strong intervention at multiple layers | Meta‑level constraints + shutdown | Halts cascade |
| Recovery Mode | Collapse reversing | Stabilization vectors + resource influx | Moves toward stability |
| Failed Containment | Containment attempts insufficient | Weak or misaligned strategies | Collapse continues |
