# TISD-Holonomy-Stability-Governance-Index-v1.0.md  
### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Meta‑Governance Metrics for Evaluating Holonomy Stability Across the Full Lifecycle

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Holonomy Stability Governance Index (HSGI)** defines the metrics used to evaluate:

- the quality of holonomy governance  
- the correctness of stability classification  
- the integrity of diagnostics  
- the responsiveness of containment  
- the sufficiency of stabilization resources  
- the honesty and survivability of the stability lifecycle  

It is the **meta‑layer** above all holonomy stability artifacts.

---

## 2. Governance Index Structure

The HSGI consists of **five governance dimensions**, each with explicit metrics:

1. **Diagnostic Governance**  
2. **Containment Governance**  
3. **Stabilization Governance**  
4. **Collapse‑Trajectory Governance**  
5. **Stability‑Label Governance**

Each dimension contributes to the overall governance score.

---

## 3. Governance Dimensions & Metrics

---

### **3.1 Diagnostic Governance**

Measures whether diagnostics are transparent, unsuppressed, and correctly interpreted.

**Metrics:**

- **D1 — Diagnostic Transparency Ratio**  
  Percentage of collapse‑relevant diagnostics visible vs. suppressed.

- **D2 — Diagnostic Responsiveness Index**  
  Speed and accuracy of responding to diagnostic changes.

- **D3 — Diagnostic Integrity Score**  
  Degree to which diagnostics match actual holonomy behavior.

**Governance Interpretation:**  
High diagnostic governance indicates honesty and survivability; low governance indicates symbolic stability or denial.

---

### **3.2 Containment Governance**

Measures the system’s ability to halt collapse propagation.

**Metrics:**

- **C1 — Containment Activation Latency**  
  Time between collapse onset and containment activation.

- **C2 — Containment Capacity Score**  
  Strength of boundaries, support, and collapse‑halt capability.

- **C3 — Containment Reliability Index**  
  Probability containment succeeds under repeated stress.

**Governance Interpretation:**  
Strong containment governance prevents CC; weak containment governance allows collapse to propagate.

---

### **3.3 Stabilization Governance**

Measures the sufficiency and continuity of stabilization resources.

**Metrics:**

- **S1 — Stabilization Resource Floor Compliance**  
  Percentage of time stabilization resources meet minimum floor.

- **S2 — Stabilization Wave Strength**  
  Magnitude and coherence of stabilization waves.

- **S3 — Stabilization Continuity Index**  
  Degree to which stabilization is sustained rather than intermittent.

**Governance Interpretation:**  
High stabilization governance supports C/AS; low governance forces downgrades to St or F.

---

### **3.4 Collapse‑Trajectory Governance**

Measures how effectively the system moves out of collapse states.

**Metrics:**

- **T1 — Collapse‑Signal Reduction Rate**  
  Speed at which collapse indicators decrease.

- **T2 — Fragmentation Resolution Score**  
  Effectiveness of resolving fragmentation signals.

- **T3 — Oscillation Suppression Index**  
  Ability to eliminate oscillatory interference.

**Governance Interpretation:**  
Strong trajectory governance indicates real recovery; weak governance indicates stagnation or hidden collapse.

---

### **3.5 Stability‑Label Governance**

Measures the honesty and correctness of stability classification.

**Metrics:**

- **L1 — Stability Label Accuracy**  
  Percentage of stability labels that match actual guarantees.

- **L2 — Stability Misclassification Penalty**  
  Severity of mislabeling (e.g., F → C).

- **L3 — Stability Revocation Responsiveness**  
  Speed of revocation when guarantees fail.

**Governance Interpretation:**  
High label governance prevents symbolic stability; low governance creates unsafe optimism.

---

## 4. Composite Governance Score

The **Holonomy Stability Governance Index Score (HSGI‑S)** is computed as:

```
HSGI-S = (D + C + S + T + L) / 5
```

Where each dimension is normalized to a 0–1 scale.

**Score Interpretation:**

- **0.85–1.00 — Exemplary Governance**  
- **0.70–0.84 — Strong Governance**  
- **0.55–0.69 — Moderate Governance**  
- **0.40–0.54 — Weak Governance**  
- **0.00–0.39 — Critical Governance Failure**

## 5. Lifecycle Integration

The Governance Index evaluates the entire lifecycle:

- **Collapse Onset** → State Machine  
- **Containment Activation** → Recovery Protocol  
- **Stabilization** → Execution Model  
- **Guarantee Establishment** → Assurance Contract  
- **Audit** → Audit Procedure  
- **Certification** → Certification Report  
- **Revocation** → Revocation Notice

The index ensures governance quality across all phases.
