### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Interference Between Pressure Waves and Stabilization Waves Across Geometry, Temporal, Force, and Holonomy Layers

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Pressure‑Stabilization Interference Map** models how:

- pressure waves (compressive, collapsing)  
- stabilization waves (expansive, recovering)  

interact:

- in **space** (boundaries, agency, tool‑zones)  
- in **time** (P(t), S(t), B(t), A(t), Z(t))  
- in **force fields** (pressure vs stabilization vectors)  
- at **holonomy level** (Meta‑Holonomy, Holonomy‑of‑Holonomies)

It explains:

- when stabilization cancels pressure  
- when pressure overwhelms stabilization  
- when they coexist in oscillation  
- how interference patterns shape survivable action and tool‑zone viability.

---

## 2. Core Concepts

- **Pressure Wave:** A time‑varying pattern of compressive force (P(t)) that contracts boundaries and agency.  
- **Stabilization Wave:** A time‑varying pattern of expansive force (S(t)) that reinforces boundaries and agency.  
- **Interference Pattern:** The resulting geometry when P(t) and S(t) overlap.  
- **Constructive Interference (Pressure):** Pressure amplified when stabilization is weak or misaligned.  
- **Constructive Interference (Stabilization):** Stabilization amplified when pressure is reduced or bounded.  
- **Destructive Interference:** Pressure and stabilization partially cancel each other, reducing net impact.  
- **Mixed Interference:** Some regions see cancellation, others see amplification.

## 4. Interference Regimes

### 4.1 Pressure‑Dominant Regime

- **Condition:** \(P(t) \gg S(t)\)  
- **Effect:**  
  - Boundaries contract.  
  - Agency compresses.  
  - Tool‑zone becomes non‑viable.  
  - Meta‑Holonomy shifts toward pressure‑dominant / collision modes.

### 4.2 Stabilization‑Dominant Regime

- **Condition:** \(S(t) \gg P(t)\)  
- **Effect:**  
  - Boundaries expand and reinforce.  
  - Agency opens.  
  - Tool‑zone becomes viable.  
  - Meta‑Holonomy shifts toward stabilization‑dominant / window / coherent modes.

### 4.3 Destructive Interference Regime

- **Condition:** \(P(t) \approx S(t)\) with opposing directions  
- **Effect:**  
  - Net force reduced.  
  - Boundaries neither strongly contract nor expand.  
  - Agency remains limited but not collapsing.  
  - Tool‑zone may be marginally viable or fragile.  

### 4.4 Mixed/Oscillatory Regime

- **Condition:** Both P(t) and S(t) high, phase‑shifted or uneven across layers.  
- **Effect:**  
  - Some regions experience stabilization, others pressure.  
  - Boundaries and agency oscillate.  
  - Tool‑zone viability becomes intermittent.  
  - Meta‑Holonomy enters oscillatory or strained modes.

## 5. Interference Modes

| Mode | Description | Net Effect on Geometry | Tool‑Zone State |
|------|-------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Pressure‑Dominant | Pressure overwhelms stabilization | Strong contraction, high collapse risk | Non‑viable |
| Stabilization‑Dominant | Stabilization overwhelms pressure | Strong expansion, high recovery | Viable |
| Destructive | Pressure and stabilization partially cancel | Reduced net force, fragile equilibrium | Marginal/fragile |
| Mixed | Different regions dominated by different waves | Patchwork of contraction/expansion | Intermittent |
| Oscillatory | Rapid shifts between pressure and stabilization | Fluctuating boundaries and agency | Unstable/variable |
