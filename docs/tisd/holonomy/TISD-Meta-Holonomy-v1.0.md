## **4. Meta‑Holonomy Interaction Model**

### **Spatial ↔ Force**
Pressure compresses geometry.  
Stabilization expands geometry.

### **Force ↔ Temporal**
Pressure and stabilization change over time.  
Temporal curves shape spatial geometry.

### **Temporal ↔ Spatial**
Boundary drift and agency oscillation reshape spatial fields.

### **Spatial ↔ Diagnostic**
Diagnostics detect boundary breaches, agency collapse, pressure spikes, stabilization windows.

### **Temporal ↔ Diagnostic**
Diagnostics detect temporal spikes, oscillations, collapses, windows.

### **Force ↔ Diagnostic**
Diagnostics detect pressure/stabilization magnitude and direction.
