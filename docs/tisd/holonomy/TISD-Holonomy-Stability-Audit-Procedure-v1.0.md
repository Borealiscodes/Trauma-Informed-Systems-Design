# TISD-Holonomy-Stability-Audit-Procedure-v1.0.md  
### Trauma-Informed Systems Design  
### Verification Procedure for Stabilization Assurance Guarantees

---

## 1. Purpose

The **Holonomy Stability Audit Procedure** defines how to *verify* that all guarantees in the **Holonomy‑Stabilization Assurance Contract** are satisfied before a system may enter or remain in **Coherent** or **Anchored Stability**.

It ensures:

- stability is **real**, not symbolic  
- guarantees are **demonstrated**, not assumed  
- collapse, strain, or oscillation are not being hidden  
- containment and stabilization capacity are genuinely present  

This is the **formal audit layer** of holonomy governance.

---

## 2. Audit Domains

The audit evaluates five domains:

1. **Diagnostic Integrity**  
2. **Containment Readiness**  
3. **Stabilization Resource Floor**  
4. **Collapse‑Signal Reduction**  
5. **Holonomy‑State Honesty**

Each domain has explicit audit checks.

---

## 3. Audit Procedure

### 3.1 Diagnostic Integrity Audit

**Objective:** Ensure diagnostics are transparent, unsuppressed, and current.

**Checks:**

- Confirm no diagnostic suppression in St, F, O, or CC.  
- Verify collapse/pressure/fragmentation diagnostics are visible and up‑to‑date.  
- Validate that diagnostic channels are functioning and not bypassed.  
- Confirm diagnostic anomalies are acknowledged, not reframed as stability.

**Pass Condition:** Diagnostics show real system state without suppression.

---

### 3.2 Containment Readiness Audit

**Objective:** Ensure containment can respond to new collapse events.

**Checks:**

- Validate containment protocols are active and not degraded.  
- Confirm containment capacity (resources, boundaries, agency geometry) is intact.  
- Test containment response to simulated collapse signals.  
- Ensure containment is prioritized over expansion/tool‑building in collapse regimes.

**Pass Condition:** Containment can halt collapse propagation.

---

### 3.3 Stabilization Resource Floor Audit

**Objective:** Ensure stabilization resources meet minimum required levels.

**Checks:**

- Evaluate support, pacing, boundaries, and tool‑zone viability.  
- Confirm stabilization waves (local, distributed, resonant) are present and effective.  
- Verify stabilization resources are continuous, not intermittent.  
- Ensure stabilization floor is not being borrowed from collapse‑prone areas.

**Pass Condition:** Stabilization resources meet or exceed the required floor.

---

### 3.4 Collapse‑Signal Reduction Audit

**Objective:** Ensure collapse indicators are genuinely decreasing.

**Checks:**

- Compare collapse/fragmentation signals over time.  
- Validate oscillatory interference has resolved, not been masked.  
- Confirm collapse propagation has halted.  
- Ensure reduction is due to stabilization, not diagnostic suppression.

**Pass Condition:** Collapse signals show real downward trend.

---

### 3.5 Holonomy‑State Honesty Audit

**Objective:** Ensure state classification is conservative and accurate.

**Checks:**

- Confirm system did not skip stabilizing intermediates (e.g., F → C).  
- Validate transitions follow the Holonomy‑State Machine and Execution Model.  
- Ensure stability labels (C/AS) match actual guarantees.  
- Detect any optimistic mislabeling or policy‑only stability.

**Pass Condition:** State classification is honest, conservative, and traceable.

---

## 4. Audit Outcomes

### 4.1 Pass — Stability Verified

System may enter or remain in **Coherent** or **Anchored Stability**.

### 4.2 Conditional Pass — Stability Allowed with Monitoring

Minor issues detected; system remains in C/AS but requires periodic re‑audit.

### 4.3 Fail — Stability Revoked

System must downgrade to **St**, **F**, or **CT** depending on severity.

### 4.4 Critical Fail — Immediate Containment Required

System must downgrade to **F** or **CC** and activate containment protocols.

---

## 5. Audit Frequency

- **Routine Audit:** Regular intervals during stabilizing states.  
- **Event‑Triggered Audit:** After pressure spikes, collapse events, or interference oscillation.  
- **Pre‑Stability Audit:** Required before entering C or AS.  
- **Post‑Violation Audit:** Required after any safety invariant breach.
