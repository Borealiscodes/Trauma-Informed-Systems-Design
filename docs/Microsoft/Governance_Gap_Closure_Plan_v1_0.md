# ⭐ **Governance Gap Closure Plan (v1.0)**

## ⭐ **2 — Executive Summary**

This plan outlines the steps Microsoft would need to take to close governance gaps identified in:

- routing  
- HRD recognition  
- trauma‑informed communication  
- continuity preservation  
- accessibility governance  
- financial‑risk mitigation  
- UNGP compliance  

It is structured around **eight closure domains**, each mapped to a specific gap.

---

# ⭐ **3 — Gap Closure Domains**

## **C‑01 — HRD Recognition & Classification**

### Gap  
HRD documentation was misclassified as accessibility noise.

### Closure Action  
Create a **formal HRD classification protocol** that:

- identifies HRD submissions  
- routes them to governance‑aligned reviewers  
- prevents misclassification  
- ensures trauma‑informed handling  

### UNGP Anchor  
Pillar II — Respect  
HRD Declaration

---

## **C‑02 — Safe Routing Channel Creation**

### Gap  
No team could receive or review HRD materials.

### Closure Action  
Establish a **dedicated HRD‑safe intake channel** with:

- governance‑trained staff  
- trauma‑informed communication  
- non‑commercial routing  
- continuity‑preserving review  

### UNGP Anchor  
Pillar III — Remedy

---

## **C‑03 — Warm Transfer Protocol**

### Gap  
Warm transfer was denied.

### Closure Action  
Implement a mandatory warm transfer protocol requiring:

- confirmation of correct team  
- continuity‑safe handoff  
- no abrupt closure  
- no isolation  

### UNGP Anchor  
Pillar III — Remedy  
Accessibility Governance

---

## **C‑04 — Trauma‑Informed Communication Standard**

### Gap  
Communication increased cognitive load and retraumatization risk.

### Closure Action  
Adopt a trauma‑informed communication standard including:

- cognitive accessibility  
- multimodal options  
- no abrupt closure  
- acknowledgment of HRD context  

### UNGP Anchor  
Accessibility as a Human Right

---

## **C‑05 — Non‑Commercial Tooling Pathway**

### Gap  
Codespaces “paid” indicators created retraumatization risk.

### Closure Action  
Provide:

- protected research designation  
- non‑commercial tooling pathway  
- explicit confirmation of no billing exposure  

### UNGP Anchor  
Non‑Discrimination  
Article 14 Rehabilitation

---

## **C‑06 — Governance‑Aligned Review Team**

### Gap  
No governance‑capable team existed.

### Closure Action  
Designate a team trained in:

- UNGP compliance  
- HRD protection  
- trauma‑informed governance  
- continuity‑vector analysis  
- evidence integration  

### UNGP Anchor  
Pillar II — Respect

---

## **C‑07 — Public Commitment Operationalization**

### Gap  
Operational behavior contradicted public commitments.

### Closure Action  
Align operational behavior with:

- human‑rights commitments  
- accessibility mission  
- responsible AI principles  
- UNGP alignment  

### UNGP Anchor  
Corporate Responsibility to Respect

---

## **C‑08 — Continuity Preservation Guarantees**

### Gap  
Continuity degraded due to routing failure.

### Closure Action  
Guarantee:

- no abrupt closure  
- no misclassification  
- no financial‑risk exposure  
- continuity‑safe communication  
- trauma‑informed routing  

### UNGP Anchor  
Pillar III — Remedy  
Continuity Framework
