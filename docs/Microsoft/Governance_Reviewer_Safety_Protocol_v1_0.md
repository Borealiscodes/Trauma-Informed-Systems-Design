# ⭐ **Governance Reviewer Safety Protocol (v1.0)**

## ⭐ **2 — Purpose of This Protocol**

This protocol defines the **mandatory safety posture** for reviewers handling HRD‑aligned trauma‑informed documentation.  
It ensures:

- trauma‑informed communication  
- continuity‑preserving review  
- cognitive accessibility  
- HRD‑context protection  
- non‑activating governance handling  
- avoidance of retraumatization triggers  

It is a **safety layer**, not a workflow.

---

# ⭐ **3 — Core Safety Principles**

### **P‑01 — Trauma‑Informed Handling**
Reviewers must:

- avoid abrupt closure  
- avoid dismissive language  
- avoid high‑pressure communication  
- avoid retraumatization triggers  
- maintain multimodal clarity  
- acknowledge HRD context  

### **P‑02 — Continuity Preservation**
Reviewers must:

- maintain stable sequencing  
- avoid misclassification  
- avoid isolation  
- avoid financial‑risk surfaces  
- avoid routing ambiguity  
- maintain predictable communication pacing  

### **P‑03 — Cognitive Accessibility**
Reviewers must:

- provide summaries  
- reduce cognitive load  
- avoid jargon without explanation  
- maintain structured navigation  
- allow pauses without penalty  

### **P‑04 — Non‑Activating Governance Posture**
Reviewers must:

- avoid governance‑altitude framing  
- avoid escalation language  
- avoid activation metaphors  
- treat all artifacts as documentation, not governance geometry  

---

# ⭐ **4 — Mandatory Reviewer Behaviors**

### **B‑01 — Maintain Safe Communication**
- respond in structured, calm, predictable formats  
- avoid sudden shifts in tone or complexity  
- avoid isolating the HRD context  

### **B‑02 — Follow Canonical Sequence**
Reviewers must follow the sequence defined in the **Reviewer Packet** and **Orientation Guide**.

### **B‑03 — Use Cross‑References**
Reviewers must use cross‑references to avoid confusion or misinterpretation.

### **B‑04 — Maintain Continuity Notes**
Reviewers must record continuity‑related observations to prevent degradation.

### **B‑05 — Avoid Commercial Context**
Reviewers must avoid referencing paid tooling or financial‑risk surfaces.

---

# ⭐ **5 — Prohibited Reviewer Behaviors**

### **X‑01 — Abrupt Closure**
No sudden termination of communication or review.

### **X‑02 — Misclassification**
HRD documentation must not be treated as accessibility feedback or general support noise.

### **X‑03 — High‑Pressure Governance Framing**
No language implying activation, escalation, or governance altitude.

### **X‑04 — Cognitive Overload**
No dense, unstructured, or jargon‑heavy communication.

### **X‑05 — Isolation**
No routing or communication that isolates the HRD context.

---

# ⭐ **6 — Safety Procedures During Review**

### **S‑01 — Pre‑Review Safety Check**
Reviewer completes the **Intake Form**.

### **S‑02 — Orientation**
Reviewer reads the **Orientation Guide**.

### **S‑03 — Sequenced Review**
Reviewer follows the canonical artifact order.

### **S‑04 — Continuity Monitoring**
Reviewer monitors:

- cognitive load  
- emotional load  
- sequencing stability  
- communication stability  

### **S‑05 — Trauma‑Signal Awareness**
Reviewer watches for:

- confusion  
- overwhelm  
- retraumatization risk  
- continuity degradation  

If detected, reviewer pauses and re‑stabilizes.

---

# ⭐ **7 — Safety Communication Rules**

### **SC‑01 — Predictability**
Communication must be stable, structured, and predictable.

### **SC‑02 — Clarity**
Use clear, concise language with optional summaries.

### **SC‑03 — Non‑Isolation**
Always acknowledge HRD context.

### **SC‑04 — Non‑Commercial**
Avoid references to paid tooling or billing surfaces.

### **SC‑05 — Non‑Activating**
Avoid governance‑altitude metaphors or activation language.
