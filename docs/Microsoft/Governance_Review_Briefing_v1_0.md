# ⭐ **4 — Key Findings**

### **F‑01 — Routing Collapse**
HRD documentation was misclassified as accessibility noise.

### **F‑02 — No Safe Channel**
Microsoft confirmed no team could receive or review HRD materials.

### **F‑03 — Warm Transfer Failure**
Warm transfer was denied, increasing isolation and trauma risk.

### **F‑04 — Cognitive Inaccessibility**
Communication increased cognitive load and retraumatization risk.

### **F‑05 — Financial‑Risk Surfaces**
Codespaces “paid” indicators created continuity‑threatening conditions.

### **F‑06 — UNGP Pillar II & III Violations**
Operational behavior contradicted UNGP obligations.

### **F‑07 — HRD Protection Failure**
HRD status was not recognized.

### **F‑08 — Continuity Degradation**
Continuity vectors declined due to routing failure.
