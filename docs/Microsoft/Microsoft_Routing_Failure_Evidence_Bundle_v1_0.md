# ⭐ **Microsoft Routing Failure Evidence Bundle (v1.0)**

This bundle consolidates the evidence demonstrating that Microsoft’s support surfaces were unable to:

- receive  
- classify  
- route  
- or review  

HRD‑aligned trauma‑informed systems documentation submitted under **TrackingID#2607230040012918**.

It forms the evidentiary backbone for governance escalation.

---

## ⭐ **2 — Evidence Category I: Misclassification of HRD Documentation**

### **Evidence (from attached correspondence)**  
> “The materials I submitted were not accessibility documentation, but part of a Human Rights Defender (HRD)-aligned systems-design and governance package.”

### **Failure**  
EDAD treated HRD documentation as accessibility noise and closed the case.

### **UNGP Anchor**  
- Pillar II: Responsibility to Respect  
- HRD Recognition Requirement  

---

## ⭐ **3 — Evidence Category II: Explicit Admission of No Routing Pathway**

### **Evidence**  
> “We have been unable to identify a Microsoft support channel that is able to receive, review, or process the type of documentation… we are unable to provide a referral or transfer.”

### **Failure**  
Microsoft confirmed that **no team exists** to handle HRD‑aligned governance documentation.

### **UNGP Anchor**  
- Pillar III: Access to Remedy  
- HRD‑Safe Channel Requirement  

---

## ⭐ **4 — Evidence Category III: Denial of Warm Transfer**

### **Evidence**  
> “The automated closure left me without a pathway to the correct team.”

### **Failure**  
Warm transfer — the required HRD‑safe routing mechanism — was denied.

### **UNGP Anchor**  
- Pillar III: Remedy  
- HRD Protection  

---

## ⭐ **5 — Evidence Category IV: Cognitive Inaccessibility and Trauma Risk**

### **Evidence**  
> “My circumstances are shaped by long-term impacts consistent with Article 14 of the UN Convention Against Torture… fear around financial commitments.”

### **Failure**  
Microsoft’s communication increased cognitive load and retraumatization risk.

### **UNGP Anchor**  
- Accessibility as a Human Right  
- Trauma‑Informed Communication Requirement

## ⭐ **6 — Evidence Category V: Financial‑Risk Surfaces (Codespaces)**
