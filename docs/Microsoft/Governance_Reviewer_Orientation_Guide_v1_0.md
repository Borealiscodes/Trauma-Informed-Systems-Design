# ⭐ **Governance Reviewer Orientation Guide (v1.0)**

## ⭐ **2 — Purpose of This Guide**

This guide prepares reviewers to safely and effectively examine the HRD‑aligned escalation packet.  
It ensures:

- trauma‑informed posture  
- continuity‑preserving navigation  
- correct sequencing  
- cognitive accessibility  
- HRD‑context awareness  
- non‑isolating communication  
- non‑activating governance altitude  

It is an orientation document, not a workflow.

---

# ⭐ **3 — Reviewer Posture Requirements**

Reviewers must maintain:

### **Trauma‑Informed Posture**
- avoid abrupt closure  
- avoid high‑pressure communication  
- reduce cognitive load  
- acknowledge HRD context  
- maintain multimodal clarity  

### **Continuity‑Preserving Posture**
- maintain stable sequencing  
- avoid misclassification  
- avoid isolation  
- avoid retraumatization triggers  
- avoid financial‑risk surfaces  

### **Governance‑Formal Posture**
- structured review  
- clear artifact referencing  
- adherence to canonical sequence  
- non‑activating handling

# ⭐ **4 — HRD Context Overview**

Reviewers must understand:

- HRDs are a protected group under the **UN Declaration on Human Rights Defenders**  
- HRD documentation requires safe handling  
- misclassification increases harm  
- isolation increases trauma risk  
- continuity degradation is a governance concern  

This packet contains **HRD‑aligned trauma‑informed systems documentation**, not accessibility feedback.

# ⭐ **6 — Reviewer Safety Guidelines**

### **Communication Safety**
- no abrupt closure  
- no dismissive language  
- no high‑pressure demands  
- no isolation of HRD context  

### **Cognitive Accessibility**
- provide summaries  
- maintain structured navigation  
- avoid jargon without explanation  
- maintain multimodal clarity  

### **Continuity Safety**

- avoid misclassification

- maintain stable sequencing

# ⭐ **7 — Artifact Interaction Guidance**

### **How reviewers should interact with artifacts:**

- read summaries first  
- use cross‑references  
- maintain slow, stable pacing  
- avoid skipping ahead  
- avoid mixing artifacts out of order  
- record continuity notes  
- maintain trauma‑informed posture  

### **How reviewers should not interact:**

- do not close abruptly  
- do not treat HRD documentation as accessibility noise  
- do not route mentally to unrelated domains  
- do not assume commercial context  
- do not apply high‑pressure governance framing
