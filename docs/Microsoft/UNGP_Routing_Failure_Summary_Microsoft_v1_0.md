- route

- trauma‑informed systems design

- evidence integration

This constitutes a **UNGP routing failure**.

## ⭐ **3 — UNGP Pillar II Failure (Responsibility to Respect)**

### Requirement  
Corporations must:

- identify human‑rights risks  
- avoid actions that silence HRDs  
- ensure dignity and respect in communication  

### Evidence (from your attached document)  
> “The materials I submitted were not accessibility documentation, but part of a Human Rights Defender (HRD)-aligned systems-design and governance package.”  
> “The automated closure left me without a pathway to the correct team.”

### Failure  
EDAD misclassified HRD documentation as accessibility noise and closed the case.

## ⭐ **4 — UNGP Pillar III Failure (Access to Remedy)**

### Requirement  
Corporations must provide:

- accessible grievance mechanisms  
- escalation pathways  
- non‑discriminatory access to remedy  

### Evidence  
> “We have been unable to identify a Microsoft support channel that is able to receive, review, or process the type of documentation… we are unable to provide a referral or transfer.”
