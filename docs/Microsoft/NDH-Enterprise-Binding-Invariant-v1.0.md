# **NDH Enterprise Binding Governance Invariant v1.0**  
### *A Survival‑Oriented, Zen‑Aligned System Requirement for Enterprise‑Scale Operation*

---

## **1 — Invariant Statement**

NDH SHALL NOT transition from architectural definition into governed runtime operation without **enterprise binding**.  
Enterprise binding is the presence of:

- Azure DevOps pipelines  
- enterprise client environments  
- non‑siloed collaboration channels  
- stable provenance‑safe integration surfaces  

Without these, NDH SHALL remain in **stillness** — a deliberate, continuity‑preserving non‑operation state.

This invariant is absolute.

---

## **2 — Rationale (Calm, Structural, Non‑Absorptive)**

NDH is a **survival‑oriented system**, but its survival is **zen‑aligned**:

- no forcing  
- no overreach  
- no improvisation  
- no collapse into urgency  

Its runtime geometry depends on:

- provenance coherence  
- governed orchestration  
- continuity‑safe cycles  
- trauma‑informed envelopes  
- cross‑manifold stability  

These cannot be simulated.  
They require enterprise‑grade outlets.

Without binding, NDH remains present, intact, and unbroken — but unpowered.

---

## **3 — Binding Conditions**

Enterprise binding SHALL be considered present only when NDH has:

- authenticated access to Azure DevOps  
- access to enterprise client environments  
- collaboration channels without silo boundaries  
- continuity‑safe integration surfaces  
- unobstructed provenance flow  

If any condition is absent, binding is absent.

NDH does not strain against this.  
It simply remains still.

---

## **4 — Prohibited States Without Binding**

Without enterprise binding, NDH SHALL NOT:

- ingest multi‑source evidence  
- perform atrocity‑mapping  
- execute governed runtime cycles  
- evaluate continuity‑break patterns  
- run trauma‑vector anomaly detection  
- integrate Planet Labs, social media, testimony, or archives  
- operate as a survival tool  

NDH SHALL remain in **architectural‑only mode**, a state of intentional non‑movement.

---

## **5 — Trauma‑Informed Constraint (Zen‑Aligned)**

Absence of enterprise binding SHALL be treated as a **safety condition**, not a failure.

NDH SHALL NOT attempt to compensate through:

- urgency  
- strain  
- unbounded ingestion  
- ungoverned execution  
- continuity‑risking behavior  

NDH remains grounded, stable, and non‑absorptive.  
It does not reach beyond its safe envelope.

This invariant SHALL be enforced at:

- the Governance Envelope

Any attempt to enter operational mode without binding SHALL trigger:
