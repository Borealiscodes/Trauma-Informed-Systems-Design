# **II — Purpose of This Case Study**

This case study examines *Gargantia on the Verdurous Planet* as a **trauma‑systems allegory**, focusing on how its narrative encodes:

- militarized indoctrination  
- dehumanization through ideology  
- moral injury  
- cultural dissonance  
- institutional betrayal  
- survivor‑centered ethical collapse  

Unlike *Shin Sekai Yori*, which centers on systemic cruelty disguised as protection, *Gargantia* centers on **ethical horror born from indoctrination and the collapse of worldview**.

---

# **III — Core Ethical Horror: Indoctrination as Identity**

The protagonist, Ledo, is raised in a militarized society where:

- obedience is absolute  
- individuality is erased  
- dissent is impossible  
- killing is normalized  
- purpose is predetermined  

The horror is not the war —  
it is the **identity architecture** built around it.

His entire sense of self is constructed by:

- military doctrine  
- hierarchical control  
- ideological purity  
- dehumanization of the enemy  
- suppression of emotion  

This is a **trauma‑informed systems failure**:  
a society that replaces humanity with function.

# **V — Trauma‑Informed Diagnostics**

## **1 — Indoctrination and Identity Collapse**

Ledo’s entire identity is built on:

- obedience  
- violence  
- hierarchy  
- purpose  
- purity  

When he arrives on Gargantia, he experiences:

- cognitive dissonance  
- moral confusion  
- emotional overload  
- identity destabilization  
- trauma‑shock  

This mirrors real‑world:

- cult exit trauma  
- military indoctrination fallout  
- authoritarian conditioning  
- survivor identity reconstruction  

The horror is not what he does —  
it is what he was **made to be**.

---

## **2 — Dehumanization of the Hideauze**

The Hideauze are framed as:

- monstrous  
- mindless  
- evil  
- existential threats  

But the truth reveals:

- they are evolved humans  
- victims of ideological conflict  
- survivors of systemic betrayal  
- targets of genocidal doctrine  

This is the ethical horror:

> Ledo was trained to exterminate a people who were once his own.

This mirrors:

- genocide logic  
- racialized dehumanization  
- ideological propaganda  
- historical revisionism  

The horror is not the war —  
it is the **lie**.

---

## **3 — Moral Injury Through Revelation**

When Ledo learns the truth, he experiences:

- guilt  
- shame  
- horror  
- betrayal  
- collapse of moral framework  

This is textbook **moral injury**:

- committing acts that violate one’s ethics  
- discovering those acts were unjustified  
- realizing one’s identity was weaponized  
- confronting the harm done to innocents  

The show’s ethical horror is that Ledo’s trauma is **structural**, not accidental.

---

## **4 — Cultural Shock Trauma**

Gargantia’s peaceful society triggers:

- emotional overload  
- sensory dissonance  
- social confusion  
- value conflict  
- existential crisis  

This is not comedic culture shock —  
it is **trauma shock**.

Ledo must confront:

- joy without purpose  
- community without hierarchy  
- life without war  
- humanity without doctrine  

The horror is that peace feels more threatening than war.

---

## **5 — Institutional Betrayal by the Galactic Alliance**

The Alliance:

- lies  
- indoctrinates  
- dehumanizes  
- weaponizes children  
- erases history  
- commits genocide  

This is the ethical backbone of the narrative.

The betrayal is total:

- moral  
- historical  
- ideological  
- existential  

The horror is not that the Alliance fails —  
it is that it **succeeds** at being monstrous.
