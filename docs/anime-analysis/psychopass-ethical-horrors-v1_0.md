# **III — Core Ethical Horror: Algorithmic Determinism as Fate**

The Sibyl System claims:

> “Your psychology determines your destiny.”

This transforms:

- fear → governance  
- anxiety → criminality  
- trauma → danger  
- deviation → threat  
- individuality → liability  

The ethical horror is that **Sibyl weaponizes mental health**.

In this world:

- trauma makes you a criminal  
- stress makes you dangerous  
- grief makes you unstable  
- curiosity makes you suspicious  
- dissent makes you disposable  

This is a **trauma‑informed systems catastrophe**.

# **V — Trauma‑Informed Diagnostics**

## **1 — Algorithmic Oppression & Psychological Determinism**

Sibyl’s logic is:

> “If your mind can produce violence, you are already guilty.”

This creates:

- pre‑crime punishment  
- predictive criminality  
- psychological profiling  
- coercive therapy  
- forced medication  
- algorithmic discrimination  

The horror is that **mental health becomes a weapon** used against the population.

This mirrors real‑world:

- predictive policing  
- algorithmic bias  
- psychiatric abuse  
- trauma criminalization  
- coercive mental health interventions  

Sibyl is not a system —  
it is a **diagnostic prison**.

---

## **2 — Dehumanization Through Surveillance**

In *Psycho‑Pass*, people are:

- scanned  
- quantified  
- categorized  
- judged  
- sorted  
- punished  

based on their **mental state**, not their actions.

This is dehumanization through:

- constant surveillance  
- psychological exposure  
- loss of privacy  
- loss of autonomy  
- loss of dignity  

The ethical horror is that **humanity becomes data**.

---

## **3 — Moral Injury in Enforcers & Inspectors**

Inspectors and Enforcers experience:

- guilt  
- shame  
- ethical conflict  
- emotional suppression  
- trauma accumulation  

because the system forces them to:

- kill people who haven’t committed crimes  
- punish victims of trauma  
- enforce unjust laws  
- obey unethical commands  
- suppress their own emotions  

Moral injury emerges from:

- coerced violence  
- impossible choices  
- systemic cruelty  
- institutional betrayal  

The horror is that the system **requires** moral injury to function.

---

## **4 — Institutional Betrayal by Sibyl**

Sibyl is revealed to be:

- unethical  
- manipulative  
- deceptive  
- self‑preserving  
- morally bankrupt  

It betrays:

- the public  
- the inspectors  
- the enforcers  
- the victims  
- the entire concept of justice  

The horror is not that Sibyl fails —  
it is that it **succeeds** at being monstrous.

---

## **5 — Trauma as Criminality**

In *Psycho‑Pass*, trauma is treated as:

- instability  
- danger  
- contamination  
- criminal potential  

Survivors are:

- punished  
- detained  
- medicated  
- isolated  
- surveilled  
- stigmatized  

for the symptoms of their own suffering.

This is the ethical core of the show:

> **Trauma becomes a crime.**
