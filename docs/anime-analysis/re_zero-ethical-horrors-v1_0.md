# **II — Purpose of This Case Study**

This case study examines *Re:Zero* as a **trauma‑systems allegory**, focusing on how its narrative encodes:

- recursive trauma loops  
- coerced resurrection  
- emotional exploitation  
- identity fragmentation  
- moral injury  
- survivor isolation  
- existential despair  
- systemic cruelty disguised as “second chances”  

Unlike Psycho‑Pass (algorithmic determinism), Gargantia (indoctrination collapse), or Shin Sekai Yori (protection‑as‑cruelty), *Re:Zero* centers on **ethical horror born from forced repetition of suffering**.

---

# **III — Core Ethical Horror: Resurrection as Coercion**

The world of *Re:Zero* frames Subaru’s ability as a gift:

> “Return by Death.”

But the ethical horror is that:

- he cannot refuse it  
- he cannot control it  
- he cannot disclose it  
- he cannot escape it  
- he cannot stop suffering  

Resurrection becomes:

- coercion  
- punishment  
- emotional torture  
- existential imprisonment  
- trauma recursion  

This is a **trauma‑informed systems catastrophe**:  
a power that forces the survivor to relive harm indefinitely.

# **V — Trauma‑Informed Diagnostics**

## **1 — Trauma Loops & Recursive Suffering**

Subaru’s trauma is:

- repeated  
- intensified  
- prolonged  
- unvalidated  
- unshared  

Each loop forces him to:

- witness death  
- experience death  
- endure despair  
- confront failure  
- relive emotional collapse  

This mirrors real‑world survivor experiences of:

- PTSD flashback cycles  
- trauma repetition  
- emotional recursion  
- helplessness loops  
- chronic moral distress  

The horror is not the death —  
it is the **forced repetition of suffering**.

---

## **2 — Coerced Resurrection & Emotional Torture**

“Return by Death” is framed as a supernatural ability, but functionally it is:

- coercive  
- non‑consensual  
- psychologically destructive  
- emotionally isolating  
- existentially imprisoning  

Subaru cannot:

- choose when to return  
- choose not to return  
- choose to disclose the ability  
- choose to escape the loop  

This is a **coercive trauma mechanism**, not a gift.

---

## **3 — Identity Fragmentation Across Timelines**

Each timeline creates:

- a different Subaru  
- a different emotional history  
- a different moral burden  
- a different psychological scar  

But only one Subaru remembers all timelines.

This creates:

- identity fragmentation  
- self‑alienation  
- existential dissonance  
- memory overload  
- emotional collapse  

The horror is that Subaru becomes **a mosaic of suffering**.

---

## **4 — Moral Injury Through Impossible Choices**

Subaru faces choices where:

- every option causes harm  
- every path leads to suffering  
- every decision violates his ethics  
- every loop forces betrayal or sacrifice  

Moral injury emerges from:

- coerced violence  
- coerced abandonment  
- coerced emotional harm  
- coerced survival at ethical cost  

The horror is that the system **requires moral injury** to progress.

---

## **5 — Survivor Isolation & Emotional Exploitation**

Subaru’s suffering is:

- unseen  
- unremembered  
- unvalidated  
- unshared  
- unacknowledged  

This creates:

- emotional isolation  
- relational dissonance  
- existential loneliness  
- despair  
- psychological collapse  

The horror is that **no one can help him**,  
because no one remembers what he endured.
