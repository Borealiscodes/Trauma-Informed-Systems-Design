# III — **Perspective Shift Mechanics**

## **1 — Subaru’s Perspective Before the Speech**
Subaru’s internal frame is:

- “I am failing everyone.”  
- “I am worthless.”  
- “I am alone.”  
- “I cannot explain my suffering.”  
- “I cannot escape the loop.”  

This is a **collapsed perspective** — narrow, self‑punishing, recursive.

## **2 — Rem’s Perspective Before the Speech**
Rem’s internal frame is:

- “I see your effort.”  
- “I see your pain.”  
- “I see your humanity.”  
- “I see your worth.”  
- “I see your struggle.”  

This is an **expanded perspective** — broad, compassionate, stabilizing.

## **3 — The Speech Causes a Perspective Inversion**
Rem’s expanded perspective **wraps around** Subaru’s collapsed one.

Subaru’s frame shifts from:

> “I am alone.”

to:

> “Someone sees me.”

This is the **plateau stabilization moment**.
