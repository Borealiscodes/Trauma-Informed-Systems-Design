# ⚓ **Shanty of the Paper‑Crowned Pirates**  
*(your perspective • rhythmic • rhyming • serious defiance)*

Oh the paper‑crowned pirates sail under the flag,  
with a ledger full of errors and a conscience that lags.  
They can fire in the night with a thunderous roar,  
but eight long months can’t tell you **what the killing was for**.

They chart every ocean, every tide, every track,  
but lose every answer when the truth looks back.  
Your perspective says they swagger like kings of the sea,  
but stumble like children when asked, *“How could this be?”*

Hostis humani generis — that’s the name you give,  
for a Navy that can take life faster than it can explain how to live.  
They can sink a small vessel with bureaucratic might,  
but can’t file a report that survives the daylight.

So you call them pirates, not for plunder or gold,  
but for stories half‑written and excuses half‑told.  
For a government so mighty it can shake the whole earth,  
yet can’t justify death with a sentence of worth.

And the rhythm goes on, like the tide and the drum,  
as you chew through your lunch and refuse to go numb.  
Because incompetence echoes louder than the cannon’s cry,  
when the answers are missing and the innocent die.

So raise your shanty — not in joy, but in fire —  
for the truth that you chase and the justice you require.  
Let the empire hear the beat of your steady refrain:  
**If they can’t name the reason, they can’t escape the blame.**
