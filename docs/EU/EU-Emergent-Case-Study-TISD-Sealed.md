# **1. Abstract**

This case study examines the emergent behavior of a multi‑layered administrative, medical, and legal system when interacting with a neurodivergent human rights defender subjected to prolonged procedural instability. Using Trauma‑Informed Systems Design (TISD) as the analytic framework, the study maps how failures across medical, asylum, academic, and regulatory subsystems produced cascading harm, culminating in supranational contradictions under EU law.

The case demonstrates how system‑level omissions (medical care, vulnerability assessment, trauma‑informed procedure) generate jurisdictional paradoxes, obstruct access to justice, and destabilize cognitive safety. It provides a novel, academically defensible model for analyzing procedural harm in complex governance environments.

# **4. Findings**

## **4.1 Systemic Omission of Trauma‑Informed Procedure**

Across all respondents, trauma‑informed requirements were absent:

- No vulnerability assessment  
- No medical evaluation  
- No trauma‑informed accommodations  
- No procedural safety  
- No cognitive‑load gating  
- No recognition of neurodivergent processing modes  

This omission created a **structural barrier** to participation.

---

## **4.2 Jurisdictional Paradox Under EU Law**

Ireland asserted that **IPAT has jurisdiction**.  
However, jurisdiction under EU law requires:

- vulnerability screening (APD 22–24)  
- medical evaluation (APD 25)  
- trauma‑informed procedure (APD 20, 24)  
- effective legal assistance (APD 19)  
- safe evidence presentation (APD 15)

None were provided.

This produced a **jurisdictional contradiction**:

> The State claims jurisdiction while failing the conditions required for jurisdiction to lawfully attach.

This contradiction is academically significant.

---

## **4.3 Emergent Harm Through Multi‑System Interaction**

The harm did not arise from a single institution.  
It emerged from **interactions between systems**:

- medical → legal  
- academic → regulatory  
- asylum → supranational  
- data protection → procedural justice  

This is a hallmark of **emergent systemic failure**.
