| Phase | Focus | Output |
|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Narrative & worldbuilding | Kid‑friendly Europa “adventure world” |
| 2 | Game logic & mechanics | 90s‑style exploration loop |
| 3 | Manifold & data scaffolding | Hidden NDH geometry under the game |

---

### Phase 1 – Narrative and worldbuilding (Europa as an adventure world)

- **Goal:** Turn Europa into a coherent, emotionally safe, curiosity‑driven world kids can explore.
- **Actions:**
  - **Define setting:** “Ice‑covered ocean moon” with caves, vents, friendly robots, and mystery.
  - **Create characters:** A Putt‑Putt‑style explorer, Europa ocean friends, instrument mascots (radar, magnetometer, camera, etc.).
  - **Establish tone:** Cozy, playful, non‑threatening; wonder, not fear.
  - **Map core regions:** Surface ice, fissures, subsurface ocean, plume zones, “science stations.”
- **Output:** A narrative bible for “Putt‑Putt Maps Europa” that kids can emotionally attach to.

---

### Phase 2 – 90s game logic and mechanics

- **Goal:** Use classic point‑and‑click adventure logic to teach manifold exploration implicitly.
- **Actions:**
  - **Design navigation:** Screen‑to‑screen movement, clickable hotspots, inventory interactions.
  - **Puzzle structure:** Fix broken paths, help characters, unlock new areas—each tied to a Europa concept (ice thickness, ocean, plumes, etc.).
  - **Feedback style:** Gentle, non‑punitive; “try again” and “let’s explore somewhere else” instead of failure.
  - **Progression:** Layered regions that gradually introduce more complex structures (from simple caves to multi‑layer ocean maps).
- **Output:** A game design document describing screens, puzzles, progression, and core loop.

---

### Phase 3 – NDH manifold and data scaffolding (under the hood)

- **Goal:** Build a hidden NDH geometry layer that the game rides on, so later we can refame it scientifically.
- **Actions:**
  - **Define manifolds:** Regions as nodes; paths as edges; loops as holonomy; anomalies as marked events.
  - **Instrument mapping:** Each “science friend” corresponds to a real Europa Clipper instrument (radar, magnetometer, cameras, spectrometers, etc.).   - **Flyby logic:** Represent different “visits” to regions as flybys; kids revisit areas with new tools or perspectives, mirroring mission passes.   [Science Mission Directorate](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/europa-clipper/)  [Science Mission Directorate](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/europa-clipper/mission-overview/)  
  - **Data abstraction:** Heat = color gradients, magnetic fields = glowing lines, ocean depth = layered visuals.
- **Output:** Internal NDH manifold spec: graph of regions, loops, anomalies, and instrument overlays.

---

### Phase 4 – Accessibility and trauma‑informed design

- **Goal:** Make the manifold emotionally and cognitively safe, especially for neurodivergent kids.
- **Actions:**
  - **Safety rules:** No time pressure, no punishment, no “game over,” no loud jump scares.
  - **Sensory design:** Calmer palettes, adjustable sound, predictable transitions.
  - **Cognitive scaffolding:** Clear goals, optional hints, visual cues for next steps, gentle repetition.
  - **Representation:** Characters that normalize curiosity, confusion, and trying again.
- **Output:** TI‑SD guidelines for kidsaccessible content + a checklist for every puzzle/scene.

---

### Phase 5 – Scientific reframing (explicit tensor calculus and rigor)

- **Goal:** Take the playful manifold and formally refame it with full scientific and mathematical rigor.
- **Actions:**
  - **Map game regions → scientific domains:**  
    - Caves → ice shell geometry  
    - Ocean zones → fluid manifolds  
    - Plumes → dynamic anomalies  
  - **Map puzzles → scientific questions:**  
    - “Fix the radar” → subsurface sounding  
    - “Help magnetometer friend” → ocean salinity and depth modeling   [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/europa-clipper/nasas-europa-clipper-millions-of-miles-down-instruments-deploying/)  
  - **Introduce math layer:**  
    - Define tensors for fields (temperature, magnetic, pressure).  
    - Define trajectories for flybys as curves on the manifold.  
    - Define holonomy for loops (tidal cycles, repeated passes).
  - **Write explicit intention:**  
    - A section in the doc stating:  
      > “This kid‑accessible manifold is intentionally designed to be refamed with full tensor calculus, NDH invariants, and mission‑grade rigor in a follow‑up scientific document.”
- **Output:** A companion “scientific refame” doc that sits alongside the kidsaccessible case study.
