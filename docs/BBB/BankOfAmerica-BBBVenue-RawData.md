## 🧭 **Which BBB region handles Bank of America?**  
Bank of America’s **headquarters BBB profile** is located in **Charlotte, North Carolina**, and is overseen by:

### **BBB of Southern Piedmont and Western North Carolina**  
  [Better Business Bureau](https://www.bbb.org/us/nc/charlotte/profile/bank/bank-of-america-0473-100421)

This is the **primary BBB region** responsible for Bank of America’s **corporate headquarters**, located at:

**100 N Tryon St STE 220  
Charlotte, NC 28202‑4031**  
  [Better Business Bureau](https://www.bbb.org/us/nc/charlotte/profile/bank/bank-of-america-0473-100421)

This is the correct BBB to use when filing a **corporate‑level complaint**, including issues involving:

- credit cards  
- corporate policies  
- systemic customer‑service failures  
- nationwide account problems  
- executive‑level escalation

## ⭐ **Which BBB should YOU use?**  
Because Bank of America is a **national credit card issuer**, and because your complaint is **not branch‑specific**, the correct venue is:

### ✅ **BBB of Southern Piedmont and Western North Carolina**  
This is the BBB responsible for **Bank of America’s headquarters**, and it is the correct place to file a **credit‑card complaint**.

---

## 📌 **Can you file a BBB complaint concurrently with a CFPB complaint?**  
Yes.  
The BBB and CFPB operate independently, and filing with both is **allowed and common**.

- **CFPB** handles regulatory enforcement.  
- **BBB** handles business‑to‑consumer dispute resolution.  

There is **no conflict** in filing both at the same time.
