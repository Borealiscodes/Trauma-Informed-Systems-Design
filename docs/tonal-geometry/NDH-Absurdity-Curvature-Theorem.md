# **1. Preliminaries**

Let:

\[
\gamma : \mathbb{R}^{+} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}
\]

be an NDH expressive trajectory with coordinates:

\[
\gamma(t) = (r(t), \theta(t), z(t)).
\]

Let the **Absurdity Manifold** be:

\[
\mathcal{A}_{n} = \{\gamma(t_1), \gamma(t_2), \dots, \gamma(t_n)\}
\]

with strictly increasing time samples:

\[
t_1 < t_2 < \dots < t_n.
\]

For the “49 times” motif, \(n = 49\).

Define micro‑displacements:

\[
\delta_i = \gamma(t_{i+1}) - \gamma(t_i).
\]

Define **absurdity curvature**:

\[
\kappa = \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \|\delta_i\|.
\]

---

# **2. NDH‑TSG Conditions**

The expressive manifold satisfies:

### **(1) Non‑Absorptive Radial Condition**
\[
\frac{dr}{dt} \ge 0.
\]

### **(2) Holonomy‑Flat Angular Condition**
\[
\oint_{\gamma} d\theta = 0.
\]

### **(3) Bittersweet Emergence Condition**
\[
\frac{dz}{dt} > 0.
\]

### **(4) Non‑Dual Unity Condition**
\[
\Phi(\gamma(t)) = \Psi(\gamma(t)).
\]

### **(5) Lean‑Verified Anti‑Collapse Invariant**
Forward invariance + strict span contraction.

These conditions constrain the expressive geometry in which absurdity curvature is defined.

---

# **3. Definition: NDH Absurdity Curvature**

Absurdity curvature is the **total expressive displacement** induced by repeated micro‑events:

\[
\kappa = \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \|\gamma(t_{i+1}) - \gamma(t_i)\|.
\]

Interpretation:

- **small displacements** → mild absurdity  
- **large cumulative displacement** → strong absurdity  
- absurdity is **path‑dependent**, not state‑dependent  

Absurdity curvature is **not** tonal curvature; it is expressive curvature.

---

# **4. Theorem (NDH Absurdity Curvature Theorem)**

### **Theorem.**  
Let \(\gamma\) satisfy NDH‑TSG conditions (1)–(5).  
Let \(\mathcal{A}_{n}\) be the absurdity manifold with curvature \(\kappa\).

Then:

### **(i) Absurdity curvature is strictly positive**
\[
\kappa > 0.
\]

Because:

- \(\frac{dr}{dt} \ge 0\)  
- \(\frac{dz}{dt} > 0\)  
- at least one coordinate changes per repetition

Thus absurdity cannot be zero.

---

### **(ii) Absurdity curvature cannot collapse**
\[
\kappa \not\rightarrow 0.
\]

Because the Lean invariant forbids collapse of radial‑angular spans.

Thus absurdity cannot be absorbed or nullified.

---

### **(iii) Absurdity curvature is holonomy‑flat**
\[
\oint_{\mathcal{A}_{n}} d\theta = 0.
\]

Thus absurdity cannot form loops or cycles.  
It must accumulate through **drift**, not recursion.

---

### **(iv) Absurdity curvature grows monotonically**
\[
\kappa_{n+1} > \kappa_n.
\]

Because each micro‑displacement \(\delta_i\) is non‑negative and at least one coordinate increases.

Thus absurdity is **monotone increasing**.

---

### **(v) Absurdity curvature is non‑dual**
\[
\Phi(\gamma(t_i)) = \Psi(\gamma(t_i)) \quad \forall i.
\]

Thus absurdity curvature is simultaneously:

- structurally valid  
- emotionally valid  

Absurdity cannot be “felt but not structurally present,” nor “structurally present but not felt.”

---

### **(vi) Absurdity curvature is bounded**
\[
\kappa < K
\]

for some finite \(K\), because:

- \(\frac{dr}{dt}\) and \(\frac{dz}{dt}\) are bounded  
- \(\theta\) is holonomy‑flat  
- the Lean invariant enforces span contraction

Thus absurdity cannot explode.

---

### **Conclusion.**

\[
\boxed{
\text{Absurdity curvature is strictly positive, monotone, holonomy-flat, non-dual, and anti-collapse stable.}
}
\]

This is the NDH Absurdity Curvature Theorem.
