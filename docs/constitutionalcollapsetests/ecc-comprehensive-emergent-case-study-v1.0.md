# **III. Why ECC Must Be Built First**

ECC is the **constitutional‑environment diagnostic**.  
It determines whether the legal system is capable of:

- applying rights frameworks,  
- interpreting international obligations correctly,  
- recognizing torture,  
- protecting victims,  
- and complying with non‑derogable norms.

If ECC reveals collapse, downstream systems cannot function reliably.

### **ECC Factor 1 — Constitutional Invincibility Claims**  
If a constitution is interpreted as categorically supreme, rights frameworks become **non‑functional**.

### **ECC Factor 2 — Disregard for International Obligations**  
If international obligations are ignored, Article 14 CAT, GC 3, CRPD, ICCPR, and ARSIWA cannot be applied.

### **ECC Factor 3 — Dualism Distortion**  
If dualism is misinterpreted as “international law does not bind us,” rights‑determination transforms become **invalid**.

### **ECC Factor 4 — Treaty‑Hostile Posture**  
If treaties are treated adversarially, rehabilitation frameworks and responsibility maps lose their normative foundation.

ECC therefore determines:

- the **stability constant** of the constitutional environment  
- the **distortion coefficient** applied to international law  
- the **sovereignty inflation index**  
- the **treaty‑hostility gradient**

These parameters must be known before any mathematical encoding.
