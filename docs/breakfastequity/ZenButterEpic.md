# **🧈 The Zen Epic of Butter and Dawn**

## **I. The Three Butter Haikus**

**Irish Butter**  
Golden hush on toast,  
soft as rain on quiet hills—  
Ireland melts first light.

**Empty Butter Dish**  
Butter dish sits bare,  
a cold white void of longing—  
toast fears the morning.

**The Liminal Butter Zone**  
Half‑melting twilight,  
butter neither here nor gone—  
toast waits, terrified.

---

# **II. The Quantum Butter Paradox**

The Novice approached the counter, trembling.  
“Master,” he said, “the butter is both present and absent.  
My toast is trapped in uncertainty.”

The Master nodded.  
“Butter exists in a quantum shimmer,” he said.  
“It is spreadable only when you accept  
that it may never have existed at all.”

The Novice swallowed.  
“So the crime is… observation?”

The Master replied,  
“The crime is believing breakfast obeys logic.”

---

# **III. The Snek in the Butter Realm**

A soft hiss echoed from the fridge.  
The Novice turned and saw it:  
a snek, gliding through the butter realm  
with the grace of a warm whisper.

“Master,” he whispered,  
“the snek leaves trails of enlightenment.”

The Master corrected him gently.  
“No.  
It leaves trails of butter.  
Enlightenment is optional.”

The snek flicked its tongue,  
judging the Novice’s spreading technique  
with ancient reptilian wisdom.

---

# **IV. The Council of Breakfast Elders**

The Elders gathered around the table,  
each holding a ceremonial utensil.

**Elder of Toast:**  
“The Liminal Butter Zone threatens us all.”

**Elder of Eggs:**  
“It is sacred.  
Butter must contemplate its destiny.”

**Elder of Tea:**  
“We must send an envoy  
with steady hands  
and a calm heart.”

The Novice felt every eye turn toward him.

The Master whispered,  
“You were chosen the moment  
you opened the fridge  
and found nothing.”

---

# **V. The Trial of the Butterless Novice**

The Novice stood before the Council.  
His crime:  
approaching breakfast without butter.

“Explain yourself,” the Elders demanded.

“I believed the butter would be there,”  
the Novice said softly.  
“I trusted the fridge.”

A gasp rippled through the chamber.

The Master stepped forward.  
“Trust is not a condiment,” he said.  
“It must be spread with intention.”

The Council murmured.  
The trial continued.

---

# **VI. The Snek’s Testimony**

The snek slithered onto the table,  
coiling like a warm spiral of judgment.

“I have seen the Novice,”  
the snek hissed.  
“He enters the butter realm  
with fear in his heart  
and crumbs on his fingers.”

The Elders nodded gravely.

“But,” the snek continued,  
“he seeks the spread.  
He seeks the dawn.  
He is worthy.”

The chamber fell silent.

---

# **VII. The Final Spread at Dawn**

The Novice approached the toast  
as the first light crept across the counter.

The butter—  
quantum, liminal, paradoxical—  
softened in the warmth of morning.

He lifted the knife.  
He breathed.  
He spread.

The Master smiled.

The snek nodded.

The Elders bowed.

And in that moment,  
the Novice understood:

Breakfast is not a meal.  
Breakfast is a practice.  
Butter is a path.  
Dawn is the teacher.
