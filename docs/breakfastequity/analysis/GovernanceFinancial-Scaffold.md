# **📐 Governance + Financial Lines Scaffold**  
### *Structural Template for Mapping Corporate Function (Pre‑Analysis)*

---

## **1. Purpose of This Document**

This scaffold defines the structure for analyzing **financial control**, **decision‑making authority**, and **governance flows** within the corporate system surrounding the entity under study.

Where the ownership chain captures **form**, this document captures **function** — the flows of:

- capital  
- obligations  
- standards  
- operational authority  
- risk  
- compliance  

This scaffold is populated after reviewing the raw dataset at:

`docs/breakfastequity/raw/TravelodgePlusDublin-CorporateStructure.json`

---

## **2. Core Questions**

Use these questions to anchor your analysis:

- Who **controls financial flows** (capital, debt, revenue extraction)?  
- Who **sets operational standards** (brand, compliance, safety, quality)?  
- Who **holds decision rights** (policy, staffing, pricing, risk)?  
- How do governance obligations **flow downward**?  
- How do reporting obligations **flow upward**?  
- Where does governance **override** ownership?  
- Where does governance **fail to reach** the operational site?

These questions will later connect to the ownership analysis via  
**Corporate ownership chain**.
