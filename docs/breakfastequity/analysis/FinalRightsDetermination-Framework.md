# **📐 Final Rights Determination Framework**  
### *The capstone structure for concluding rights analyses in asylum‑lodging systems*

---

## **1. Purpose of This Document**

This framework provides the structure for issuing a **formal rights determination** after all upstream analyses have been completed:

- Ownership chain  
- Governance + financial flows  
- Rights synthesis  
- Government contract analysis  
- Lived‑conditions assessment  

Its purpose is to produce a **clear, defensible conclusion** about:

- which rights are upheld  
- which rights are violated  
- who is responsible  
- where accountability lies  
- what structural reforms are required  

This is the final interpretive layer of the breakfastequity system.

## **3. Rights Determination Structure**

The determination is built across five layers:

### **Layer A — Rights Baseline**
Define the rights that apply:

- statutory rights  
- constitutional rights  
- international protection rights  
- contractual rights  
- ethical rights  

This creates the baseline against which conditions are judged.

---

### **Layer B — Evidence Integration**
Integrate evidence from:

- lived conditions  
- contract obligations  
- governance flows  
- ownership structures  

For each rights domain (e.g., safety, dignity, autonomy), summarize:

- **evidence supporting compliance**  
- **evidence supporting breach**  
- **evidence showing structural vulnerability**  

---

### **Layer C — Rights Determination (Per Domain)**

For each rights domain, issue a determination:

- **Upheld** — evidence shows consistent compliance  
- **Partially upheld** — mixed evidence; structural risks present  
- **Violated** — evidence shows breach or systemic failure  
- **Severely violated** — evidence shows ongoing harm or high‑risk conditions  

Domains typically include:

- physical environment  
- safety and security  
- health and well‑being  
- autonomy and dignity  
- social conditions  
- complaint and redress mechanisms  

---

### **Layer D — Responsibility Attribution**

Using ownership + governance + contract analysis:

- identify **primary duty‑holders**  
- identify **secondary duty‑holders**  
- identify **entities insulated from consequences**  
- identify **entities with power but no obligation**  
- identify **entities with obligation but no power**  

This is where structural accountability is made explicit.

---

### **Layer E — Structural Reform Recommendations**

For each rights breach or vulnerability:

- identify the **entity responsible**  
- identify the **governance or financial lever**  
- propose **contractual reforms**  
- propose **operational reforms**  
- propose **oversight reforms**  
- propose **reporting and transparency reforms**  

This section becomes the actionable output of the entire pipeline.

- synthesizes all findings
