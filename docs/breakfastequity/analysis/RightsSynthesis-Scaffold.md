# **📐 Rights Synthesis Scaffold**  
### *Structural Template for Integrating Ownership, Governance, and Rights Obligations*

---

## **1. Purpose of This Document**

This scaffold provides the structure for synthesizing:

- the **corporate ownership chain**  
- the **governance + financial lines**  
- the **rights obligations** relevant to the entity under study  

Its purpose is to produce a unified rights‑focused analysis that identifies:

- responsibility  
- leverage  
- accountability  
- structural inequity  
- rights vulnerabilities  

This synthesis becomes the core analytic engine for later work on asylum lodging contracts, regulatory obligations, or any rights‑sensitive domain.

---

## **2. Inputs Required**

Populate this scaffold only after completing:

- **Ownership Chain Analysis**  
- **Governance + Financial Lines Analysis**  

Optional (but recommended):

- Contractual obligations  
- Regulatory requirements  
- Lived‑conditions data  
- Inspection or compliance reports  

---

## **3. Synthesis Framework Overview**

The synthesis integrates three axes:

### **Axis A — Structural Form (Ownership)**  
- Who owns the entities?  
- How does ownership cascade?  
- Where does ownership concentrate or diffuse responsibility?

### **Axis B — Structural Function (Governance + Financial Control)**  
- Who makes decisions?  
- Who controls money?  
- Who sets standards and enforces compliance?

### **Axis C — Rights Obligations (Legal + Ethical)**  
- What rights must be protected?  
- What duties exist under law, contract, or policy?  
- Where do obligations fail to reach the operational site?

The synthesis identifies where these axes align — and where they conflict.

## **6. Rights Vulnerability Analysis**

Document where rights are structurally fragile:

- governance gaps  
- enforcement failures  
- misaligned incentives  
- opaque reporting lines  
- fragmented decision rights  
- financial extraction that undermines care  
- ownership structures that diffuse accountability  

This section becomes the diagnostic engine for rights‑based evaluation.
