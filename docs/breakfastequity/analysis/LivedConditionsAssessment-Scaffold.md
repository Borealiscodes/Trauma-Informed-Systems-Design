# **📐 Lived‑Conditions Assessment Scaffold**  
### *Structural Template for Evaluating On‑the‑Ground Rights Conditions in Asylum Lodging*

---

## **1. Purpose of This Document**

This scaffold provides a structured method for assessing **real‑world conditions** experienced by asylum seekers in contracted lodging environments.  
It integrates:

- operational realities  
- environmental conditions  
- safety and dignity factors  
- service delivery quality  
- rights vulnerabilities  

It is designed to plug directly into:

- **Rights synthesis**  
- **Government contract analysis**  

---

## **2. Inputs Required**

Populate this scaffold using:

- site inspection reports  
- lived‑experience testimonies  
- NGO or oversight findings  
- complaint logs  
- regulatory assessments  
- contract obligations (from your GovContract scaffold)  
- operational data from the governance analysis  

---

## **3. Assessment Domains**

Evaluate lived conditions across the following domains:

### **A. Physical Environment**
- room conditions  
- cleanliness  
- overcrowding  
- ventilation and heating  
- accessibility  
- privacy  

### **B. Safety & Security**
- staff presence  
- incident reporting  
- emergency procedures  
- harassment or violence risks  
- protection for vulnerable groups  

### **C. Health & Well‑Being**
- access to medical care  
- mental‑health support  
- nutrition quality  
- sanitation  
- infection control  

### **D. Autonomy & Dignity**
- freedom of movement  
- ability to make personal choices  
- respect for cultural practices  
- treatment by staff  
- access to communication  

### **E. Social Conditions**
- community integration  
- isolation risks  
- access to education  
- access to legal support  
- opportunities for recreation  

### **F. Complaint & Redress Mechanisms**
- availability  
- accessibility  
- responsiveness  
- retaliation risks  
- escalation pathways
