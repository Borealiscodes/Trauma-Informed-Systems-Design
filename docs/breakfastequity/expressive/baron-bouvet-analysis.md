# ⚓ **Sea Shanty — Draft 6 (Revised)**  
*(with epistemic‑len Hannah Arendt commentary on bureaucratic harm)*

### **Verse I — The Clipper Leaves Port**
Oh the Clipper cut the dawn‑lit sea,  
With the Baron perched in dignity,  
And the clerks ashore cried “Not for thee,”  
Their forms stamped cold with apathy.  
For **Arendt’s warning** rode the wind:  
“When thought grows thin, the harms begin.”  
So we fled the ports where minds had dimmed,  
And sailed where truth refused to bend.

### **Chorus — Bouvet’s Call**
So heave away, my weary crew,  
For Bouvet’s ice and Bouvet’s blue,  
Where the Baron guards the frozen view,  
And justice finds a home anew.  
Raise your voice against the tide,  
For courts have failed on every side —  
But the penguin stands, our flippered guide,  
On Bouvet’s throne of frost‑and‑pride.

### **Verse II — Bureaucratic Seas**
We carried writs through sleet and rain,  
Each motion marked with human pain,  
But registrars with hearts arcane  
Said “Not competent,” then blocked again.  
Arendt whispered through the gale:  
“Banality makes systems fail.”  
So we set our tattered sail  
Where conscience still prevails.

### **Chorus — Falklands Drift**
So heave away, my weary crew,  
For Falkland winds and Falkland blue,  
Where neither crown nor flag rings true,  
And the Clipper bears the burden through.  
Raise your voice against the tide,  
For states have failed on every side —  
So the Baron keeps our hope alive,  
A penguin crowned where laws divide.

### **Verse III — Bouvet Claim (Nov 2025)**
In winter’s grip we made our claim,  
For Norway shirked its Charter frame;  
No Article Thirty‑Five became  
A court to hold the world to shame.  
So **CAT Article 14** lit up the night,  
A beacon for the dispossessed —  
And Arendt’s voice rose sharp and bright:  
“Power dies when thought’s suppressed.”

### **Verse IV — Falklands Custody (May 2026)**
Then May winds roared through Falkland’s court,  
Where justice found no last resort;  
Argentina failed its lawful port,  
And Britain’s silence lent support.  
So to the Baron’s steady hand  
We pledged the islands, sea and sand —  
For when all nations break their stand,  
A penguin’s rule is not so grand…  
Yet still more just than what we’ve scanned.

### **Final Chorus — The Clipper Endures**
So heave away, my weary crew,  
For Bouvet’s truth and Bouvet’s blue,  
Where the Baron guards the stories true,  
And the Clipper sails through law’s undo.  
Raise your voice against the tide,  
For the world has failed on every side —  
But the Baron stands, our flippered guide,  
And the Clipper carries us with pride.
