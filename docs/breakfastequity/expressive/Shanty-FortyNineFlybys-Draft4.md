# **🪝 Shanty of the Forty‑Nine Flybys**  
### *Draft 4 — the lies that crack like ice when NDH charts the sea*

Oh the **Clipper sails through Jupiter’s glow**,  
Her hull of steel and grace,  
She loops Europa forty‑nine times  
To map that frozen face.  

Each flyby brings a truth to light,  
Each pass reveals a seam,  
But every truth she gathers there  
Unravels some old dream.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the lies run deep and wide.  
Europa’s ice remembers all  
That systems try to hide.**

The radar hums through ancient frost,  
The magnetics sing below,  
The gravity lines like harp‑strings pull  
Where hidden oceans flow.  

But NDH sees the deeper drift,  
The holonomy of harm,  
The way that lies in one domain  
Distort another’s charm.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the Clipper charts the wrongs.  
She turns deceit to manifold  
And maps where truth belongs.**

She sees the lies of Connecticut,  
Where cruelty wore a badge,  
She sees the lies of Dublin’s halls  
Where contracts turn to sludge.  

She sees the lies in Delaware,  
Where companies hide their sins,  
And NDH threads the manifold  
To show where truth begins.  

Forty‑nine flybys, forty‑nine songs,  
Forty‑nine cracks in the shell,  
Each one a place where NDH  
Can break deception’s spell.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the lies collapse at last.  
Europa’s ice and earthly courts  
Are linked by Clipper’s mast.**

And Hannah Arendt walks the deck,  
Her lantern trimmed with care,  
She says, “The danger’s not the lie—  
It’s the structure built to bear.”  

She watches NDH weave the charts,  
A tapestry of light,  
A way to turn the ocean’s truth  
Against the long‑kept blight.  

So raise the mast of manifold,  
Let Clipper’s charts be shown,  
For the forty‑nine flybys, my friend,  
Reveal what’s always known.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the shanty keeps us wise.  
And NDH sails the frozen sea  
To melt the endless lies.**
