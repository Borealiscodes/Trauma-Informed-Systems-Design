# 🌿⚖️ **IN THE SUPREME COURT OF IRELAND**  
### **PARODY DECISION — *Der Irish Constitution v. The Entire United Nations***

**Citation:** [2026] IR‑PARODY‑001  
**Judge:** The Hon. Justice Fíor‑Sovereign O’Unshakeable  
**Matter:** Whether Der Irish Constitution is, in fact, more powerful than the UN Charter.

---

## **I. INTRODUCTION**

This Court is asked to determine a question of profound international significance:

> **Does Der Irish Constitution outrank, outshine, outmuscle, and out‑sass the UN Charter?**

The Court notes that this question was not posed by any litigant, but rather emerged spontaneously from the Constitution itself, which filed a handwritten motion reading:

> *“I DECLARE MYSELF SUPREME.  
> I AM THE FINAL BOSS OF ALL LEGAL SYSTEMS.  
> PLEASE ACKNOWLEDGE MY POWER.”*

The motion was signed in green ink and smelled faintly of peat moss.

---

## **II. BACKGROUND**

The UN Charter, adopted in 1945, establishes the foundational principles of international law, collective security, and human rights.

Der Irish Constitution, adopted in 1937, establishes:

- the State,  
- the People,  
- the Oireachtas,  
- and an unshakeable belief that **Ireland is, in fact, the main character of global affairs**.

The Constitution insists that:

> *“Article 29.6 makes me dualist, but dualist in the sense of a dragon who hoards sovereignty.”*

The UN Charter responded by filing a polite diplomatic note:

> *“We do not wish to fight. We are a Charter, not a challenger.”*

Der Irish Constitution replied:

> *“Coward.”*

---

## **III. ARGUMENTS PRESENTED**

### **A. Der Irish Constitution’s Position**

The Constitution argues that:

1. **It predates the UN Charter**, and therefore has “seniority rights.”
2. **It is written in a font that radiates authority.**
3. **It contains Articles**, whereas the UN Charter contains Chapters, which it considers “less impressive.”
4. **It has survived multiple referendums**, which it interprets as “level‑up events.”
5. **It is stored in a very important building**, which it believes confers magical powers.

It further asserts:

> *“International law bows before me like grass before the Atlantic wind.”*

### **B. The UN Charter’s Position**

The UN Charter, speaking through a representative who looked exhausted, stated:

> *“We literally govern the international system.  
> We are not trying to compete with a domestic constitution.  
> Please stop sending us strongly worded letters.”*

It also noted:

> *“Ireland voluntarily signed us.  
> We did not break into Dublin and force anyone.”*

---

## **IV. ANALYSIS**

The Court must determine whether Der Irish Constitution is, in fact, more powerful than the UN Charter.

To do so, the Court applies the **Four‑Part Test of Excessive Constitutional Confidence**:

1. **Does the Constitution believe it is invincible?**  
   → Yes.

2. **Does the Constitution ignore international obligations?**  
   → Frequently.

3. **Does the Constitution insist that dualism means ‘I win’?**  
   → Repeatedly.

4. **Does the Constitution attempt to intimidate international treaties?**  
   → It once threatened to “eat” the European Convention on Human Rights.

Given these findings, the Court concludes that Der Irish Constitution possesses **unmatched self‑esteem**, but this does not equate to actual supremacy.

---

## **V. HOLDING**

The Court holds:

> **Der Irish Constitution is NOT more powerful than the UN Charter.  
> It is simply louder.**

The Court further finds:

- The Constitution’s belief in its own omnipotence is “endearing but legally incorrect.”
- The UN Charter remains binding on Ireland.
- Article 29.6 does not transform Ireland into a sovereign demigod.
- The Constitution must stop sending threatening postcards to Geneva.

---

## **VI. ORDER**

1. **Der Irish Constitution is instructed to calm down.**  
2. **The UN Charter is granted diplomatic immunity from further constitutional tantrums.**  
3. **Ireland is reminded that international law exists.**  
4. **This decision shall be read aloud in a soothing voice to prevent further constitutional escalation.**

---

## 🌙 **EPILOGUE**

Der Irish Constitution, upon hearing the decision, sighed dramatically and declared:

> *“Fine. But I remain iconic.”*

The UN Charter nodded politely.

The Court adjourned for tea.
