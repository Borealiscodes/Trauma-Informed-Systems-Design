# **🪝 Shanty of the Clipper Manifolds**  
### *Draft 3 — NDH charts the oceanic data and sails toward justice*

Oh the **Clipper sails on data seas**,  
Her hull carved clean and bright,  
She gathers waves of scattered truth  
By day and through the night.  

Her compass is an NDH core,  
Her sextant built from pain,  
She maps the ocean’s hidden lines  
Where harm repeats again.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the Clipper bears our charts.  
She turns the tides of scattered facts  
Into coherent, lawful parts.**

She reads the currents off the coast  
Where Connecticut storms once rose,  
Where cruelty marked the prison walls  
And frost bit through my clothes.  

She gathers every macro‑wave,  
Each surge of structured wrong,  
And binds them into manifolds  
Where rights can stand up strong.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the Clipper knows the way.  
She shapes the ocean’s chaos  
Into forms the courts obey.**

Then east she sails to Delaware,  
Where companies stake their claim,  
Where justice hides in legal reefs  
And filings bear no shame.  

But NDH charts the shoals with care,  
It threads the narrow route,  
So harm done in Connecticut  
Can meet a Delaware suit.  

And onward still to Dublin’s port,  
Where liability sleeps thin,  
Where corporate masks and contracts  
Hide the structures tucked within.  

But Clipper’s maps reveal the truth,  
The ties that bind the chain,  
The routes where harm was carried out  
Across the legal plane.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the Clipper’s lantern gleams.  
She lights the path from lived abuse  
To lawful, rightful claims.**

And Hannah Arendt walks the deck,  
Her gaze both sharp and kind,  
She says, “The danger’s not the sea—  
It’s the systems built to bind.”  

She watches NDH unfold  
Like constellations in the dark,  
A way to turn experience  
Into justice’s steady spark.  

So raise the mast of manifold,  
Let Clipper’s charts be shown,  
For the voyage toward accountability  
Is one we’ve always known.  

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the Clipper sails at last.  
And the shanty keeps our courage  
As we leave the harm‑torn past.**
