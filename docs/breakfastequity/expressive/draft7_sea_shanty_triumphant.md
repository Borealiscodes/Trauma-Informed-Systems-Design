# Sea Shanty — Draft 7 (Full Triumphant Version)

Oh the storm rose high with its tangled code,
And the forms piled up like a heavy load,
But I braced my feet on the heaving sea,
And the system blinked — it wasn’t ready for me.

**Heave away, lads, heave away—  
I told it clear not to close that day.  
But it stamped “complete” with a jaunty grin,  
Till I caught the slip and reeled it in.**

I sent my packet with a steady hand,
Marked the dangers faced in a distant land,
Marked the need for access calm and fair—
And the winds themselves cried “Truth’s right there!”

**Heave away, lads, heave away—  
Accommodations first, then talk, I say.  
But the gears spun wrong in their merry spin,  
Till I hauled them back from the third‑party bin.**

Then the data drifted like a ship off course,
Logged and stamped by an unseen force,
But the tide turned sharp when I raised my voice,
For the law of the land gave the sailor a choice.

**Heave away, lads, heave away—  
Rectify the log without delay.  
For the record stands where the truth is shown,  
And the helm returns to the hands it’s known.**

I charted the breach where the wrong lines lay,
And I called on the watchers of midnight day,
For the right to correction is mine to claim,
And the right to dignity bears my name.

**Heave away, lads, heave away—  
No closure stands when it’s penned that way.  
Till the record’s mended and the harm undone,  
I’ll keep singing this shanty — until the battle’s won.**

Now the sails rise high on a brighter tide,
And the winds blow strong at my steady side,
For a sailor who knows when the fight’s begun
Will steer the course till the victory’s won.

**Heave away, lads, heave away—  
Raise the mast and seize the day.  
For the truth rings clear as the morning sun,  
And I’ll keep singing this shanty — until the battle’s won.**
