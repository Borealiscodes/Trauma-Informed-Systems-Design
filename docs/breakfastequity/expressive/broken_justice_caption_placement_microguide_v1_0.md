# ⚖️🖋️ **Caption Placement Micro‑Guide (v1.0)**  
### *How to place the chosen poetic line beneath the broken justice diagram*

This micro‑guide ensures the caption you select from the decision matrix sits beneath the diagram in a way that:

- preserves emotional containment,  
- maintains expressive‑lane tone,  
- reinforces symbolic clarity,  
- and avoids visual or narrative overload.

It is intentionally short — a placement rule, not a full commentary.

---

## ⭐ 1 — Caption Positioning  
Place the caption **directly beneath the diagram**, with:

- **one line of vertical space** above it,  
- **no indentation**,  
- **no bolding**,  
- **no italics**,  
- **no quotation marks**.

This keeps the caption feeling like a *whisper beneath the image*, not a headline.

---

## ⭐ 2 — Caption Length  
Use **one sentence only**.

A caption should be:

- concise,  
- symbolic,  
- emotionally neutral,  
- and non‑absorbing.

Long captions pull the viewer out of the diagram’s geometry.

---

## ⭐ 3 — Caption Tone  
The caption should:

- describe the system,  
- not the person,  
- not the harm,  
- not the experience.

It should name **structural incoherence**, not emotional impact.

This is why the recommended captions focus on:

- forgetting purpose,  
- broken justice,  
- incoherence as habit,  
- impossible navigation,  
- opacity of process.

---

## ⭐ 4 — Caption Alignment  
Center the caption **only if** the diagram is centered.

If the diagram is left‑aligned, keep the caption left‑aligned.

Consistency maintains expressive coherence.

---

## ⭐ 5 — Caption Spacing  
Add **one horizontal rule** (`---`) beneath the caption if the page continues with text.

This creates a gentle boundary between:

- the visual artifact,  
- the poetic line,  
- and the next section.

---

## ⭐ 6 — Caption Emotional Altitude  
The caption must remain:

- **non‑absorbing**,  
- **non‑activating**,  
- **symbolic**,  
- **structural**,  
- **contained**.

If a caption feels too heavy, choose one with higher emotional altitude from the decision matrix.

---

## ⭐ 7 — Caption Finalization Rule  
Once placed, **do not annotate the caption**.

No footnotes.  
No explanations.  
No references.

The caption must stand alone as a single interpretive gesture.
