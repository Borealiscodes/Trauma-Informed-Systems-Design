# **⚓ “The Jaunty Shanty of the Usual Way” — Storm‑Swagger Edition**  
### *A sea shanty that kicks the door in on procedural rigidity*

> **(Verse 1)**  
> Oh the brass‑bound machinery chants “the usual way,”  
> As if tempests take orders or trauma obeys.  
> The ship cuts a line through bureaucratic gray,  
> While truth in the rigging shouts wild through the haze.

> **(Chorus)**  
> **Sing HEY! Let the ship give way—**  
> **For the sea won’t roll in the usual way.**  
> **When lived human weather refuses their cues,**  
> **We bellow a storm‑born shanty the system can’t use.**

> **(Verse 2)**  
> The waves slap the hull with a salt‑bitten warning,  
> For storms never bow to a calendar’s beat.  
> The ocean laughs loud at procedural morning,  
> And “usual” snaps clean beneath sailors’ feet.

> **(Chorus)**  
> **Sing HEY! Let the ship give way—**  
> **For the sea won’t roll in the usual way.**  
> **When lived human weather refuses their cues,**  
> **We bellow a storm‑born shanty the system can’t use.**

> **(Verse 3)**  
> Arendt cried caution at banality’s treason:  
> When process pretends it’s the truth of the sea.  
> When harm is dismissed as a glitch without reason,  
> And lived human storms are denied formally.

> **(Bridge)**  
> So hoist up the lanterns of rightful correction,  
> Let rectification roar fierce through the mast.  
> For truth is a compass that cuts through deflection,  
> And lived human stories outlast what is cast.

> **(Final Chorus)**  
> **Sing HEY! Let the record display**  
> **What the storm carved deep in its furious sway.**  
> **When “the usual way” denies what is true,**  
> **We roar a sea shanty no system can subdue.**

---
Provenance: This expressive artifact was created within the Trauma-Informed-
Systems-Design framework to illustrate how lived human realities challenge
systems that rely on "the usual way." It uses maritime narrative, defiant tone,
and Arendtian epistemic critique to highlight the limits of procedural cadence
when confronted with trauma, accessibility barriers, and complex lived
experience.

This shanty is part of the breakfastequity expressive lane, supporting narrative
equity, trauma-informed communication, and expressive epistemology across TISD.
