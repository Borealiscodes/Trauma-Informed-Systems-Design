# **⚓ “Support Is Available (But Not Actually)”**  
### *A sarcastic sea shanty with tight rhythm and maritime bite*

> **(Verse 1)**  
> Oh they shout from the dock, “Aye, support is available!”  
> Like a seagull squawking slogans it learned yesterday.  
> They toss you a line made of words unmistakable—  
> Then watch it snap clean in the very first wave.

> **(Chorus)**  
> **Yo‑ho! What a comforting say—**  
> **“Support is available,” sure, in a brochure way.**  
> **But when the storm hits hard and you’re battered through,**  
> **Their “support” is a postcard they forgot to send you.**

> **(Verse 2)**  
> They swear there’s a raft, “Aye, support is available!”  
> But it’s patched with disclaimers and duct‑taped with forms.  
> They gesture real wide, looking ever so capable—  
> While you’re out here alone in the teeth of the storm.

> **(Chorus)**  
> **Yo‑ho! What a comforting say—**  
> **“Support is available,” sure, in a brochure way.**  
> **But when the storm hits hard and you’re battered through,**  
> **Their “support” is a postcard they forgot to send you.**

> **(Verse 3)**  
> They chant the same line, “Aye, support is available!”  
> Like a ship’s bell stuck ringing the same empty note.  
> It’s the kind of refrain that sounds almost relatable—  
> Till you notice it’s tied to a half‑sinking boat.

> **(Bridge)**  
> So raise up the sails of the help that is *actual*,  
> Not the scripted assurance they toss from the pier.  
> For lived human storms need more than contractual—  
> They need support real enough actually to appear.

> **(Final Chorus)**  
> **Yo‑ho! Let the record display—**  
> **Their “support is available” drifts miles away.**  
> **When the sea gets rough and the truth comes through,**  
> **We sing a sharp shanty they can’t spin into “support.”**
