# ⚓ **“The Shanty of the Illinois Accreditor”**  
### *A jaunty, defiant, rhyming shanty for the BS ahead*

> **Oh the HLC sails in the land of corn,**  
> **Where the statutes bite and the laws are born,**  
> **But they’ll dodge and weave with a practiced yawn—**  
> **“Just fill out the form,” at the break of dawn.**  
>  
> **They’ll pretend your torture was a clerical slip,**  
> **And your ADA packet a wandering ship,**  
> **But Illinois law’s got a cracking whip—**  
> **And it snaps when they try that accreditation quip.**  
>  
> **They’ll call it “grievance,” small and slight,**  
> **Though the record’s wrong and the harm’s in sight,**  
> **But the dignity clause is a lantern bright—**  
> **And it burns through the fog of their bureaucratic night.**  
>  
> **They’ll claim your filings are too immense,**  
> **But the Remedy Clause makes a sharp offense,**  
> **For denying access is consequence—**  
> **And Illinois courts do not spare pretense.**  
>  
> **So let HLC send their scripted reply,**  
> **With a checkbox grin and a paper‑thin lie,**  
> **For the moment they try to wave you by—**  
> **The jurisdictional tide will rise sky‑high.**  
>  
> **Raise your voice with jaunty might,**  
> **For the law is wind and the truth is right,**  
> **And the accreditor’s dodge is a doomed delight—**  
> **When Illinois storms in with constitutional bite.**
