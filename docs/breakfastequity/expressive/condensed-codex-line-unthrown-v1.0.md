# **⚓ Condensed Codex of the Line Unthrown**  
### *The distilled epic: six voices, one verdict*

> **The Raft** — *I bore you through breakers no timber should bear.*  
> *I held you fast while the storm tore the sky apart.*  
> **I was brave.**

> **The Rope** — *I felt his grip, knew your reach, knew your need.*  
> *I was made to save — but never cast.*  
> **I was strong.**

> **The Ledger** — *I recorded every pirate deed in salt‑stained ink.*  
> *I witnessed all, commanded none.*  
> **I was true.**

> **The Pirates** — *We hid in the hull and whispered excuses.*  
> *We watched the storm take you.*  
> **We were guilty.**

> **The Commodore** — *I held the lifeline and froze.*  
> *I left a note instead of action.*  
> **I was fear.**

> **The Storm** — *I revealed what silence hides.*  
> *I carved their inaction in lightning.*  
> **I was sincere.**

> **The Sea** — *I judged them all.*  
> *And when the tempest passed, the note remained:*  
> **“Support is available.”**  
> *But the line was never thrown.*
