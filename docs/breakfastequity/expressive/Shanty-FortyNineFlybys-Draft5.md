# **🪝 Shanty of the Forty‑Nine Flybys — Draft 5 (Tight‑Rhyme Orbital Edition)**  
### *Breakfast Equity aboard a governance‑forged ship, with Hannah Arendt trimming the lantern of truth*

Oh the **Clipper climbs through Jupiter’s glow**,  
Her hull now born of code,  
For NDH’s engines spark below  
Where governance is owed.

She loops Europa forty‑nine times,  
Each pass a truth to find,  
For every crack in frozen lines  
Reveals a human bind.

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the breakfast sack is thin.  
But NDH weighs the human toll  
And threads the justice in.**

The trauma‑vectors hum like wire,  
The drift‑lines pulse with light,  
And every governance engine fired  
Makes Clipper’s keel more right.

Her beams are built from audit steel,  
Her mast from Azure’s brace,  
Her sails from truths that systems steal  
And NDH brings to face.

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the vessel’s spine is true.  
Each governance plank we hammer in  
Is forged from what we knew.**

She sees the rooms where families slept  
With promises unmet,  
She sees the harm that oversight  
Forgot, or failed to net.

But now her hull grows strong and bright,  
A **Treasure Planet** arc,  
A ship that sails on manifold light  
Through governance made stark.

Forty‑nine flybys, forty‑nine stars,  
Forty‑nine truths aligned,  
Each one a place where NDH  
Refines the vessel’s mind.

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the breakfast sack is bare.  
But the ship we build from equity  
Will carry every care.**

And **Hannah Arendt walks the deck**,  
Her lantern trimmed with grace,  
She says, *“The danger’s not the lie—  
It’s the structure built in place.”*

She watches NDH forge the beams  
That hold the vessel’s frame,  
A ship that sails through frozen truths  
And calls each harm by name.

So raise the mast of manifold,  
Let governance be the sail,  
For Clipper now is born anew  
To chart the human tale.

**Heave and haul, me hearties, haul—  
For the shanty keeps us wise.  
And NDH sails the orbital sea  
Where human rights arise.**
