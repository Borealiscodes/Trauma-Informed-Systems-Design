## **Re: Gentle Inquiry Into Whether I Am Still on Your “Special Communications Arrangement” List**

Tēnā koutou,

I hope this message finds you well, and that your inbox is enjoying a peaceful, unencumbered existence — unlike mine, which is currently hosting several courts, a few governments, and the occasional emergency petition.

I am writing with a small, polite, and hopefully non‑disruptive question:

### **Am I still blacklisted?**

On **18 July 2025**, the Commission informed me that:

> “From 18 July 2025, the Commission will only receive and consider communication from you if it is sent in writing by post to PO Box 6751, Auckland 1142…”

This arrangement was to last **12 months**, after which it would be reviewed.

As we are now well past that period, I am simply seeking clarification on whether:

1. **The restriction has been lifted**,  
2. **I may once again communicate through normal channels**,  
3. **Or whether I remain on the Commission’s ‘special communications diet’**,  
   consisting exclusively of postal mail and existential patience.

I ask this in good faith, and with full awareness that GANHRI standards encourage NHRIs to:

- support human rights defenders,  
- avoid retaliatory restrictions,  
- and maintain accessible communication pathways.

I am sure the Commission strives to uphold these principles, and I would be grateful for confirmation that the 2025 restriction has now sunsetted gracefully into the archives.

---

## **Why I Need to Know**

I am currently involved in urgent proceedings before the **New Zealand Court of Appeal**, where the question of whether I have access to domestic human rights mechanisms is… well… relevant.

It would be helpful to know whether:

- I may cite the Commission as an accessible NHRI,  
- or whether I must continue describing it as “available only by postal mail, provided one has stamps, stability, and a functioning state.”

---

## **A Polite Request for Clarity**

Could you please confirm:

- whether the July 18, 2025 restriction is still in force,  
- whether email communication is now permitted,  
- and whether any new communication arrangements apply?

I promise to adhere to whatever the current policy is — even if it requires carrier pigeons, semaphore flags, or interpretive dance.
