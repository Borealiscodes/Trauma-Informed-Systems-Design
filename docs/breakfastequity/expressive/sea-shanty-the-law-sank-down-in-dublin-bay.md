# ⚓ **“The Law Sank Down in Dublin Bay” — Salt‑of‑the‑Earth Shanty (v2, with real chorus)**

> **Oh the tide rolled in from Dublin Bay,**  
> *Yo‑ho, haul away,*  
> **With a Section 18 notice sayin’ “Nay, lad, nay,”**  
> *Yo‑ho, haul away.*  
>  
> **“You’re no worker in our ledgers, so your truth can’t touch the shore,”**  
> *Yo‑ho, haul away,*  
> **“And the treaties that you carry won’t be honored anymore,”**  
> *Yo‑ho, haul away.*

## ⚓ **CHORUS — The Strong One**

> **So heave, lads, heave, let the broken law go free,**  
> **Let it sink beneath the waves where the blind courts cannot see,**  
> **For the tide will take the truth where the judges fear to stray —**  
> **And we’ll sing it as we haul, through the dark of Dublin Bay.**
