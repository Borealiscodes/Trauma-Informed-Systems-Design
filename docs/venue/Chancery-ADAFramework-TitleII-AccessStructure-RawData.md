## 1. What the Delaware Courts ADA framework actually says

From the **ADA Public Notice** and related forms, we can extract the core structural commitments.   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/aoc/ada.aspx)  [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=115068)  

### 1.1 Non‑discrimination commitment (Title II)

- **Scope:** Delaware Judiciary will not discriminate against qualified individuals with disabilities in **services, programs, or activities**.  
- **Legal basis:** Title II of the ADA (1990).  
- **Limits:** No obligation to take actions that **fundamentally alter** programs or impose **undue financial/administrative burden**.   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/aoc/ada.aspx)  

### 1.2 Accommodation commitments

- **Reasonable and necessary accommodations** must be granted to qualified persons with disabilities.  
- Judiciary will provide **appropriate aids and services** for effective communication (e.g., sign language interpreters, accessible formats).   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/aoc/ada.aspx)  

### 1.3 Accommodation request process

- Requests should be made **as far in advance as possible**; may take up to **15 business days** to process.  
- Court ADA Coordinator must **confirm receipt within 5 business days** and decide (approve/deny/modify) within **15 business days**.  
- Some accommodations that **impact proceedings** (extensions, adjournments, testimony modifications) may need to be **filed with the court** and decided by the judicial officer.   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/aoc/ada.aspx)  

### 1.4 Grievance and appeal structure

- **ADA Grievance Form**: used to request review of a denied or modified accommodation decision; Court ADA Coordinator can provide **alternative submission methods** if needed.   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=115068)  
- **ADA Accommodation Appeal Form**: escalates grievances to the **Administrative Office of the Courts (AOC) ADA Coordinator**; alternative submission methods available; contact via **InfoADA@delaware.gov**.   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=115078)  

### 1.5 Email and alternative access channels

- **InfoADA@delaware.gov** is explicitly listed as a contact for ADA appeals and coordination.   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=115078)  
- ADA materials state that **alternative methods of submitting grievances/appeals** can be provided when needed (e.g., if standard written submission is inaccessible).   [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=115078)  [Delaware Courts](https://courts.delaware.gov/forms/download.aspx?id=115068)  

This is the key: **ADA pathways explicitly contemplate non‑standard, alternative submission methods**, including email and other accessible formats.
