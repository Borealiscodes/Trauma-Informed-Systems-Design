#### 5.3. NDH + ZenAI combined intervention

Now apply NDH/TISD/Zen AI Design as **constraints and design posture** on top of ZenAI.

**Step 1: Model the workflow with GBS**

- **Manifold**: all possible paths a survivor might take through the institution.  
- **Tensor**: multi-dimensional representation of:
  - Channels (email, phone, web, in-person).  
  - Actors (survivor, staff, AI agents).  
  - States (intake, assessment, decision, appeal).  
- **Vector**: actual trajectory of a given case.  
- **Scalar**: misalignment metrics:
  - Number of times story is repeated.  
  - Number of channel switches.  
  - Number of unexplained delays.  
- **Envelope**: stability bounds:
  - Maximum acceptable cognitive load.  
  - Maximum acceptable uncertainty.  
- **Holonomy**: loops where survivors are forced to re-advocate or re-explain.  
- **Output**: a trauma-safe, predictable case journey.

**Step 2: Encode NDH invariants into ZenAI configuration**

- **Role-based access**:
  - Survivors’ sensitive data only visible to specific roles.  
  - AI agents restricted from exporting data to external LLMs.  

- **Agentic workflows**:
  - Intake agent must:
    - Capture story once.  
    - Generate a canonical case narrative.  
    - Ensure all downstream agents use that canonical narrative.  

- **Interaction patterns (from Zen AI Design Interaction Library)**:
  - Single-channel communication enforced:
    - Survivors choose a primary channel.  
    - System commits to that channel unless explicitly changed.  
  - Predictable steps:
    - AI-generated timelines and expectations.  
    - Clear explanation of rights and appeal paths.  

- **Koan: Missing Icons (mirror)**:
  - Used to audit:
    - UI elements that mislead or confuse.  
    - Missing indicators of status, rights, or next steps.  

**Step 3: Train staff with Zen AI Design Training Module**

- Staff learn:
  - How to use ZenAI agents **without violating NDH invariants**.  
  - How to recognise and correct drift in AI-generated outputs.  
  - How to maintain dignity and predictability even under pressure.

**Result:**

- ZenAI becomes:
  - Not just an AI platform,  
  - but a **trauma-informed AI substrate**, governed by NDH/TISD.
