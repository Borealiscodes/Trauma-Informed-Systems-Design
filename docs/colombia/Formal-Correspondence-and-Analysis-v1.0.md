# **II. English Translation — Clean Version**

```
Respected Sir/Madam:

You are kindly greeted by the Integrated Center for Citizen Attention and Relations – GCIARC of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. We respectfully refer to the request you submitted to this Ministry, in which you sent a communication in a language other than Spanish.

In this regard, and in accordance with Article 17 of Law 1755 of 2015, we inform you that we require more information about the procedure or information you are requesting from this Ministry. Therefore, we need you to indicate the following:

In accordance with Article 17 of Law 1755 of 2015, we reiterate the requirement for more information about the procedure or information you are requesting from this Ministry. Therefore, we need you to indicate the following:

1. Submit your request in Spanish, in accordance with Article 10 of the Political Constitution, Law 2 of 1960 (“which establishes measures for the defense of the national language”), and Decree 189 of 1964, whose Article 2 states: “The following shall be expressed in the Spanish or Castilian language: a) Official documents issued by authorities, and petitions addressed to them.”

Finally, the petitioner is reminded that from this moment, you have one (1) month as the deadline to complete your request; otherwise, it will be closed due to tacit withdrawal, in accordance with the Law.

The petitioner is kindly asked to send their response to this requirement to the inbox contactenos@cancilleria.gov.co, indicating in the subject line the filing number 1908056-CO.

Cordially,
```
