# “The Bureaucracy of Bogotá Bay”
## A Dramatic, Jaunty Sea Shanty of Endless Paperwork and Procedural Doom
### (Expressive Documentation Artifact)

Oh gather ‘round, ye sailors grim,
And hear a tale of fate so dim,
Of Bogotá Bay where clerks hold sway,
And drown all souls in forms all day.

Heave ho, ye wanderers, heave ho,
Through tides of ink and undertow,
For every slip you sign at sea
Returns with stamps — in triplicate, three!

They say the Bay’s a cursed place,
Where time itself slows down its pace,
And every sailor brave and free
Is trapped by the Great Bureaucracy.

CHORUS:
Yo‑ho, the Bureaucracy, yo‑ho,
Where papers rise and spirits low,
For every form you file today,
They’ll send it back another way.

The clerks stand tall with quills in hand,
A fearsome, ink‑soaked, ghostly band,
They chant in halls of marble cold:
“Submit again — your form’s not bold!”

And sailors cry, “We wrote it clear!”
But echoes mock them far and near,
“Your language, sir, is not our own —
Return tomorrow, then beg the throne!”

CHORUS:
Yo‑ho, the clerks of Bogotá,
They’ll bind your ship in legal straw,
And every stamp they slam with glee
Adds one more year to your decree.

The captain tried to plead his case,
But ink exploded in his face,
And from the rafters came the roar:
“New radicado — file once more!”

The bosun cursed, the deckhands wept,
The navigator nearly slept,
For every chart they tried to read
Was blocked by some procedural creed.

CHORUS:
Yo‑ho, the Bureaucracy tide,
Where hope itself is cast aside,
And sailors brave who seek the shore
Are handed forms forevermore.

The waves grew thick with stamped replies,
The sky went dark with office cries,
And every gull that dared to fly
Was forced to fill Form 45‑I.

But still the crew held fast and true,
“We’ll weather this,” they swore anew,
“For though the clerks may block our way,
We’ll sing them down in Bogotá Bay!”

FINAL CHORUS:
Yo‑ho, the Bureaucracy, yo‑ho,
We’ve braved the storm and undertow,
And though the clerks may rise again,
We’ll meet them with a sailor’s grin.
