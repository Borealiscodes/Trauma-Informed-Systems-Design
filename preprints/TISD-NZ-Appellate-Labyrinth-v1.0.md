VI. Treaty Conflict Analysis

A. UNCAT Article 13
Right to complain without obstruction.  
Obstruction documented:
- filing refusal  
- form rigidity  
- fee requirement  
- postal‑only communication (NZHRC)  
- no oral accommodation  

B. UNCAT Article 14
Right to redress and rehabilitation.  
Conflict:
- redress contingent on filing  
- redress contingent on fee  
- redress contingent on procedural capacity  
- time‑bar defeats remedy  

C. CRPD Articles 11 & 13
Protection in emergencies; access to justice.  
Conflict:
- no accommodations  
- no oral informal application  
- no remote filing pathway  
- no disability‑sensitive procedure  

D. VCLT Articles 26–27
Treaties must be performed in good faith; domestic law cannot justify breach.  
Conflict:
- domestic procedural rules used to override treaty obligations  

E. ARSIWA
State responsibility for internationally wrongful acts.  
Conflict:
- continuing failure to provide redress  
- continuing procedural obstruction  
- continuing disability discrimination
