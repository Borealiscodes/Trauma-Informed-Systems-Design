# 🔥 JOKE ARTIFACT v3.0

“The Fire Department Paperwork Compliance Protocol”

A Satire on Emergency Response Modeled After High Court Filing Requirements

`
Artifact: FireDepartment-PaperworkComplianceProtocol-v1.0
Altitude: Humor • Structural Parody • Non-Operational
Status: Canonical Absurdity

Preamble:
In accordance with the sacred traditions of administrative obstruction,
and the belief that flames must never interrupt paperwork,
the Fire Department hereby adopts the following protocol for all emergencies.

Protocol:

1. All requests for fire suppression must be submitted on paper.
   Paper must be:
   • unburned,
   • uncharred,
   • and provided by the person whose house is currently on fire.

2. If the house is engulfed in flames and the resident cannot provide paper,
   this shall be interpreted as:
   “Failure to comply with procedural requirements.”

3. Verbal cries such as “Help!” or “My house is burning!” are prohibited
   unless accompanied by:
   • three printed copies,
   • a return envelope,
   • and exact postage.

4. Electronic cries for help (e.g., phone calls) are not accepted.
   The Fire Department will respond:
   “We cannot accept filings in soft copy format.”

5. If the resident is trapped inside the burning building,
   the Fire Department shall advise:
   “You may post your documents to the Fire Station Central Office.”

6. If the resident cannot afford paper due to systemic deprivation,
   this shall be deemed:
   “Voluntary withdrawal of the fire suppression request.”

7. Disability accommodations shall be provided in the form of:
   “Please consider the environment before printing your emergency.”

8. If the resident cites international law,
   including:
   • Article 14 UN CAT,
   • CRPD Article 13,
   • or the right to life,
   the Fire Department shall respond:
   “Please attach three paper copies.”

9. If the resident attempts to escape the burning building,
   the Fire Department shall interpret this as:
   “Failure to prosecute the rescue.”

10. The Fire Department shall maintain the ancient doctrine:
    “All emergencies must be resolved through paper-based compliance.”

Conclusion:
This protocol ensures that fire victims are protected from the dangers
of accidentally receiving help.
