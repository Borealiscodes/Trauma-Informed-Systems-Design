# 🎭 JOKE ARTIFACT: The Paper‑Based Justice Protocol

Version 1.0 • Humor Altitude • Non‑Normative • Procedural Absurdity Module

`
Artifact: Paper-Filing-Parody-v1.0
Altitude: RP • Humor • Structural-Only
Status: Dormant

Preamble:
In recognition of the sacred traditions of administrative inconvenience,
and the centuries-old belief that justice cannot exist unless printed on
A4 paper purchased at personal expense, the following protocol is hereby
invoked whenever a destitute torture survivor attempts to file anything.

Protocol:
1. All filings must be submitted on paper.
   Paper must be:
   • uncreased,
   • untraumatized,
   • and purchased by the applicant using money they do not have.

2. If the applicant is homeless, medically neglected, or unable to afford
   paper, the Court shall interpret this as:
   “Failure to comply with procedural requirements.”

3. Electronic filings are prohibited because:
   • they are too accessible,
   • too efficient,
   • and might accidentally allow justice to occur.

4. Disability accommodations shall be provided in the form of:
   “We regret to inform you that we cannot accept filings in soft copy.”

5. Emergency injunctions may be submitted orally,
   but only if the applicant first:
   • prints the oral request,
   • binds it,
   • and delivers it in person,
   • during business hours,
   • while not being homeless.

6. If the applicant cites Article 14 UN CAT,
   the Court shall respond:
   “Please attach three paper copies.”

7. If the applicant cannot afford paper due to torture,
   medical deprivation,
   or systemic exclusion,
   the Court shall advise:
   “You may post your documents along with copies and a return envelope.”

8. If the applicant cannot afford a return envelope,
   this shall be deemed:
   “Voluntary withdrawal of the application.”

9. All filings must be accompanied by a solemn oath:
   “I swear I have complied with all procedural requirements,
    except the ones made impossible by the State.”

10. The Court shall maintain the ancient doctrine of:
    “Justice is available to all who can afford stationery.”

Conclusion:
This protocol ensures that victims of torture, medical neglect,
and transnational repression are protected from the dangers of
accidentally accessing justice.
`

🜂 ANNOTATION TILE (Humor Altitude)
- This artifact is satire.  
- It exposes the absurdity of demanding paper filings from someone who cannot afford paper because the State caused the destitution.  
- It mirrors the exact contradiction you’re living: a system that requires resources it itself has denied you.  
- It is structurally safe, non‑activating, and emotionally accurate.

and 16(4), which require accessible, trauma-informed, and non-discriminatory pathways 
to justice.

as de facto denial of justice for vulnerable individuals. Serves as a reflective and 
archival artifact within TISD, illustrating systemic absurdities and reinforcing the 
analysis of procedural exclusion across Irish institutions.
