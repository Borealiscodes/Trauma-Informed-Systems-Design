“The Lifeguard Procedural Compliance Protocol”

A Satire on Paper‑Based Justice Applied to Drowning Victims

`

Altitude: Humor • Structural Parody • Non-Operational
Status: Canonical Absurdity

Preamble:
In recognition of the sacred administrative traditions of inaccessible justice,
and the belief that emergencies must never interrupt paperwork,
the following protocol governs all lifeguard responses to drowning victims.

Protocol:

1. All rescue requests must be submitted on paper.
   Paper must be:
   • dry,
   • legible,
   • and provided by the drowning person.

2. If the drowning person cannot provide paper due to “being underwater,”
   this shall be interpreted as:
   “Failure to comply with filing requirements.”

3. Verbal cries for help are prohibited unless accompanied by:
   • three printed copies,
   • a return envelope,
   • and a €2 stamp.

4. Electronic cries for help (e.g., screaming) are not accepted.
   The lifeguard will respond:
   “We cannot accept filings in soft copy format.”

5. If the drowning person is unconscious,
   the lifeguard shall advise:
   “You may post your documents to the Lifeguard Central Office.”

6. If the drowning person cannot afford paper due to systemic deprivation,
   this shall be deemed:
   “Voluntary withdrawal of the rescue request.”

7. Disability accommodations shall be provided in the form of:
   “Please consider the environment before printing your emergency.”

8. If the drowning person cites international law,
   including:
   • Article 14 UN CAT,
   • CRPD Article 13,
   • or the right to life,
   the lifeguard shall respond:
   “Please attach three paper copies.”

9. If the drowning person sinks below the surface,
   the lifeguard shall interpret this as:
   “Failure to prosecute the rescue.”

10. The lifeguard shall maintain the ancient doctrine:
    “All emergencies must be resolved through paper-based compliance.”

Conclusion:
This protocol ensures that drowning victims are protected from the dangers
of accidentally receiving help.
