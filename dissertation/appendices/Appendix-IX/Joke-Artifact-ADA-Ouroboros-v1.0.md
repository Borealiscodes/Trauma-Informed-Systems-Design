III. Structural Analysis (Mock‑Serious)
This diagram demonstrates the emergence of a self‑validating administrative paradox, wherein:

1. The Supreme Court proves the accommodation is reasonable  
   by granting it.

2. The Mandamus Petition becomes structurally correct  
   because the Supreme Court’s action shows the Chancery Court’s refusal was unreasonable.

3. The Chancery Court’s refusal becomes evidence  
   because the Supreme Court’s acceptance disproves their claim of impossibility.

4. The ADA request becomes recursive  
   because you needed email filing to request email filing.

5. The entire system becomes a case study  
   for your dissertation’s thesis that bureaucracy can behave like a crime against humanity.
