# ⭐ 2. Structure of a Systems‑Design Koan  
A systems‑design koan has three parts:

### **A. The Anchor**  
A simple, grounded domain concept.

Examples:  
- mapping  
- routing  
- identity  
- continuity

- invariants  

### **B. The Compression**  
A minimal question that collapses complexity.

Examples:  
- “Does it map.”  
- “Where does it route.”

- “What breaks the loop.”  
- “What is the boundary.”

The cognitive shift produced by the question.
