- issues urgent appeals,

- mobilizes civil‑society networks.

4. FLD can escalate to EU and UN bodies
FLD has established channels with:

- UN Special Rapporteurs,  
- EU human‑rights desks,  
- European Parliament committees,  
- international NGOs.

Your case is now positioned for multi‑layered escalation if Ireland fails to act lawfully.
