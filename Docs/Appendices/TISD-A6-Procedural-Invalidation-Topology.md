# TISD‑A6 — Procedural Invalidation Topology

Trauma‑Informed Systems Design (TISD) Topological Appendix

This appendix formalizes the topology of procedural invalidation — how institutions reshape, collapse, or disconnect the “communication manifold” of trauma‑affected individuals.  
It models invalidation not as a semantic act, but as a topological transformation that removes continuity, breaks paths, and destroys connectedness.

The goal:  
detect invalidation through topology, not narrative.

---

A6.1 Communication Manifold Definition

Let:

- \( \mathcal{T} \) = trauma‑affected communication  
- \( \mathcal{I} \) = institutions  
- \( \mathcal{P} \) = procedures  

Define the communication manifold:

\[
\mathcal{M}_{\text{comm}} = (\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{I}, \mathcal{P})
\]

This manifold encodes:

- continuity of communication  
- connectedness between institutions  
- stability of meaning  
- accessibility of procedural pathways  

---

A6.2 Invalidation Operator

Define the invalidation operator:

\[
\mathcal{V} : \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}}
\]

Where:

\[
\mathcal{V}(t) = \emptyset
\]

for trauma‑affected communication \( t \in \mathcal{T} \).

Interpretation:

- “Your complaint is difficult to discern”  
- “We cannot accept atypical filings”  
- “This is not within our mandate”  
- “Postal only”  
- “We cannot be responsible for deadlines”  

Each invalidation collapses part of the manifold.

---

A6.3 Topological Collapse

Define collapse:

\[
C : \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}}
\]

Where:

\[
C(\mathcal{T}) = \text{point}
\]

This models:

- flattening of communication  
- erasure of nuance  
- removal of trauma context  
- reduction of complex evidence to “non‑compliant”  

CIDT emerges when:

\[
\| C \| \gg 0
\]

---

A6.4 Disconnection of Procedural Paths

Define path disconnection:

\[
\pi : \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}} \rightarrow \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}}
\]

Where:

\[
\pi(\gamma) = \emptyset
\]

for communication paths \( \gamma \) that rely on:

- accommodations  
- atypical formats  
- trauma‑affected expression  
- fragmented evidence  

Interpretation:

- oral applications rejected  
- remote filings denied  
- atypical submissions invalidated  
- communication channels restricted  

This breaks the manifold’s connectedness.

---

A6.5 Invalidation Homology Groups

Define homology:

\[
Hk(\mathcal{M}{\text{comm}}) = \text{communication holes}
\]

Invalidation creates holes in the manifold:

- missing evidence  
- missing context  
- missing procedural access  
- missing rehabilitation  

CIDT emerges when:

\[
\dim H_k \gg 0
\]

---

A6.6 Trauma‑Affected Topological Distortion

Define distortion:

\[
D(\mathcal{M}{\text{comm}}) = \int{\mathcal{T}} \| \mathcal{V}(t) \| \, dt
\]

Interpretation:

- invalidation distorts communication  
- trauma amplifies distortion  
- inaccessible procedures increase distortion  

CIDT emerges when:

\[
D \geq D_{\text{CIDT}}
\]

---

A6.7 Multi‑Agency Topological Composition

Define composite manifold:

\[
\mathcal{M}{\text{system}} = \bigcup{k=1}^{n} \mathcal{M}{\text{comm}}^{(Ik)}
\]

Where each institution contributes a communication manifold.

CIDT emerges when:

\[
\mathcal{V}{\text{system}} = \bigcup{k=1}^{n} \mathcal{V}{Ik}
\]

produces:

- large collapse  
- high distortion  
- many holes  
- disconnected paths  

---

A6.8 Invalidation Connectivity Index (ICI)

Define:

\[
\text{ICI} = \frac{\text{number of disconnected components}}{\text{total communication components}}
\]

Interpretation:

- high ICI = systemic invalidation  
- low ICI = isolated procedural issue  

Threshold:

\[
\text{ICI} \geq \tau_{\text{CIDT}} \Rightarrow \text{CIDT detected}
\]

---

A6.9 Summary of Invalidation Topology Signatures

Procedural invalidation is present when:

- communication collapses  
- paths disconnect  
- homology groups expand  
- distortion increases  
- trauma amplification exceeds thresholds  
- composite manifolds accumulate holes  
- ICI exceeds CIDT thresholds  

These signatures are mathematically detectable.
