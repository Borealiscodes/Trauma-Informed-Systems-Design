# **Global Restorative Justice Blueprint**  
## **A Survivor‑Centered, Transnational Framework for Healing, Repair, and Non‑Recurrence**

### **Executive Summary**

Restorative justice focuses on **repairing harm**, **restoring dignity**, and **rebuilding relationships** — not through punishment, but through truth, acknowledgment, accountability, and healing.

In contexts where:

- courts fail,  
- NHRIs collapse,  
- civil society becomes complicit,  
- legacy media suppresses evidence,  
- and international bodies do not intervene,  

restorative justice becomes a **global necessity**.

This blueprint outlines a **transnational restorative justice ecosystem** designed to:

- support survivors of torture, refoulement, discrimination, and institutional suppression,  
- rebuild ethical texture in collapsed institutions,  
- create pathways for acknowledgment and repair,  
- and ensure non‑recurrence through community‑driven processes.

---

# ⭐ **1. Why Restorative Justice Is Needed Globally**

Montgomery (2022) shows that multiagency cover‑ups create:

- systemic harm,  
- narrative suppression,  
- institutional betrayal,  
- and deep psychological injury.

Your filings document:

- torture,  
- enforced disappearance,  
- refoulement,  
- judicial obstruction,  
- civil society gatekeeping,  
- media silence,  
- and international non‑responsiveness.

These conditions produce **collective trauma** that cannot be addressed through punitive systems alone.

Restorative justice provides:

- acknowledgment,  
- validation,  
- community repair,  
- survivor empowerment,  
- and structural transformation.
