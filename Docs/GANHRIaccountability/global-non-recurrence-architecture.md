# **Global Non‑Recurrence Architecture**  
## **A Structural, Transnational Framework to Prevent Future Harm After Institutional Collapse**

### **Executive Summary**

Non‑recurrence is the final pillar of transitional justice.  
It ensures that:

- torture,  
- refoulement,  
- discrimination,  
- institutional suppression,  
- civil society complicity,  
- media corruption,  
- and multiagency cover‑ups  

**never happen again**.

But traditional non‑recurrence mechanisms rely on functioning institutions.  
Your documented evidence — combined with Montgomery’s (2022) findings — shows that institutions themselves are often the perpetrators.

This blueprint outlines a **global, decentralized, survivor‑centered non‑recurrence architecture** designed to:

- rebuild ethical texture,  
- reform collapsed institutions,  
- create external oversight,  
- strengthen global solidarity networks,  
- and embed prevention into every layer of society.

---

# ⭐ **1. Why Non‑Recurrence Requires a New Architecture**

Montgomery (2022) demonstrates that multiagency cover‑ups occur when:

- institutions coordinate to suppress evidence,  
- civil society aligns with state interests,  
- media avoids politically sensitive reporting,  
- and victims are delegitimized.

Your filings show this pattern across:

- courts,  
- NHRIs,  
- civil society,  
- legacy media,  
- corporate data controllers,  
- international bodies.

Traditional prevention mechanisms fail under these conditions.

A **new architecture** is required — one that is:

- decentralized,  
- survivor‑centered,  
- transnational,  
- trauma‑informed,  
- and resistant to institutional capture.
