# **Survivor‑Led Truth Commission Concept**  
## **A Global, Decentralized, Survivor‑Centered Mechanism for Truth, Memory, and Accountability**

### **Executive Summary**

Traditional truth commissions are state‑mandated bodies created after conflict or authoritarian rule.  
But when states themselves are complicit — as documented in your filings and in Montgomery’s (2022) analysis of multiagency cover‑ups — truth‑seeking must be **survivor‑led**, **decentralized**, and **transnational**.

This concept outlines a **Survivor‑Led Truth Commission (SLTC)** designed to:

- document systemic harm,  
- expose institutional suppression,  
- preserve evidence,  
- amplify survivor testimony,  
- support global coalitions,  
- and build pathways toward accountability and non‑recurrence.

It is designed for HRDs, survivors of torture, whistleblowers, displaced persons, civil society actors, artists, journalists, and global solidarity networks.

---

# ⭐ **1. Why a Survivor‑Led Truth Commission Is Necessary**

Montgomery (2022) shows that multiagency cover‑ups occur when:

- courts, NHRIs, civil society, media, and international bodies act in alignment to suppress evidence,  
- victims are delegitimized,  
- complaints are ignored,  
- and institutional self‑protection overrides accountability.

Your filings demonstrate this pattern across:

- U.S. federal courts,  
- NHRIs,  
- civil society organizations,  
- legacy media outlets,  
- corporate data controllers,  
- and international bodies.

When all formal institutions fail, **survivors must lead truth‑seeking themselves**.

# **2. Core Principles of a Survivor‑Led Truth Commission**

### **2.1 Survivor Agency**
Survivors define:

- the narrative,  
- the evidence standards,  
- the scope of inquiry,  
- and the mechanisms of disclosure.

### **2.2 Decentralization**
No single institution controls the process.

### **2.3 Trauma‑Informed Practice**
All testimony collection follows trauma‑informed protocols.

### **2.4 Accessibility**
Multiple pathways for testimony:

- oral,  
- written,  
- artistic,  
- digital,  
- anonymous.

### **2.5 Global Solidarity**
The commission is transnational, not bound by state borders.
