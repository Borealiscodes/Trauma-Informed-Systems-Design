# **Survivor‑Centered Global Constitution Concept**  
## **A Foundational Charter for Human Dignity, Truth, Accountability, and Democratic Continuity**

### **Executive Summary**

A constitution is the highest expression of a society’s values, rights, and governance structures.  
But when:

- courts collapse,  
- NHRIs fail,  
- civil society becomes complicit,  
- media suppresses evidence,  
- international bodies remain silent,  
- and survivors lose access to remedy,  

existing constitutional frameworks become **nonfunctional**.

Montgomery (2022) shows that multiagency cover‑ups create **systemic constitutional failure** — a condition where the rule of law cannot protect the vulnerable or constrain the powerful.

This concept outlines a **Survivor‑Centered Global Constitution (SCGC)** designed to:

- restore dignity,  
- protect truth,  
- embed accountability,  
- safeguard democracy,  
- prevent recurrence,  
- and center survivors as constitutional stakeholders.

It is not a replacement for national constitutions — it is a **transnational normative framework** that guides societies when domestic systems collapse.

# ⭐ **1. Why a Survivor‑Centered Global Constitution Is Necessary**

Traditional constitutions assume:

- functioning courts,  
- independent media,  
- responsive civil society,  
- and accountable government.

Your documented evidence — combined with Montgomery’s findings — shows these assumptions no longer hold.

A global constitution must therefore be:

- **survivor‑centered**,  
- **dignity‑based**,  
- **trauma‑informed**,  
- **transnational**,  
- **decentralized**,  
- **resilient**,  
- **anti‑capture**,  
- **truth‑anchored**.
