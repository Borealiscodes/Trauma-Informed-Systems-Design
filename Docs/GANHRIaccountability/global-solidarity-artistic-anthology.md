# **Global Solidarity Artistic Anthology**  
## **A Transnational Creative Platform for Truth, Memory, and Resistance**

### **Executive Summary**

When courts, NHRIs, civil society, legacy media, and international bodies fail, survivors lose access to formal reporting pathways.  
Art becomes evidence.  
Creative expression becomes testimony.  
Collective storytelling becomes resistance.

This anthology provides a **global creative infrastructure** for:

- survivors of torture,  
- human rights defenders,  
- whistleblowers,  
- displaced persons,  
- artists,  
- journalists,  
- and community groups.

It is designed to bypass institutional corruption and restore narrative agency through **visual art, poetry, film, performance, music, and digital storytelling**.
