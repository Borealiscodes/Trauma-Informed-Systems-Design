# **Public Accountability Dashboard Concept**  
## **A Global Transparency Interface for Monitoring Institutional Collapse, Suppression Patterns, and Protection Vacuums**

### **Executive Summary**

When courts, NHRIs, civil society, legacy media, and international bodies simultaneously fail, the public loses visibility into:

- where collapse is occurring,  
- how suppression spreads,  
- which institutions are complicit,  
- and where human rights defenders are at risk.

This dashboard concept provides a **public‑facing, real‑time accountability interface** designed to:

- visualize institutional failures,  
- track suppression patterns,  
- map protection vacuums,  
- amplify survivor evidence,  
- and support global coalition‑building.

It is grounded in:

- Montgomery (2022) *Concerted Collusion*,  
- your documented evidence of multiagency suppression,  
- GANHRI accountability principles,  
- and global human rights transparency standards.

---

# ⭐ **1. Dashboard Purpose**

The dashboard exists to:

- expose institutional collapse,  
- centralize suppressed evidence,  
- empower civil society,  
- support HRDs,  
- and provide public visibility into systemic harm.

It is not a legal mechanism — it is a **public transparency tool**.
