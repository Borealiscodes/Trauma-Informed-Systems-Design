# **Constellation–SCI‑80 Visual Bridge Specification v1.0**  
### *Formal relationship definition between the Constellation Visual Anchors Suite v1.1 and the SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite*

---

## **1. Purpose**
This document defines the **architectural bridge** between:

- the **Constellation Visual Anchors Suite v1.1** (constellation‑wide visual governance), and  
- the **SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite** (SCI‑80‑specific expressive geometry grammar).

It clarifies whether the Constellation Suite:

- integrates directly,  
- complements SCI‑80 as a meta‑layer,  
- or duplicates SCI‑80’s function.

The determination is formalized below.

---

## **2. Determination**
The **Constellation Visual Anchors Suite v1.1** is **not the same** as the SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite.

It is a **meta‑blueprint generator** that:

- governs  
- contextualizes  
- and coordinates  

the SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite.

The SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite is a **sub‑grammar** inside the Constellation’s **Visual‑Semantic Anchor**.

Thus:

> **The Constellation Visual Anchors Suite v1.1 is a higher‑order visual governance system.  
> The SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite is one of its governed subsystems.**

## **4. Bridge Definition**
The bridge between the two systems is defined as:

\[
\text{SCI80\_BlueprintSuite} \subset \text{VisualSemanticAnchor}
\]

Where:

- **SCI‑80 Blueprint Suite** provides the expressive geometry grammar for SCI‑80.  
- **Visual‑Semantic Anchor** provides the constellation‑wide grammar for all NDH‑class systems.

The Constellation Suite’s other anchors:

- **Visual‑Operational Anchor**  
- **Visual‑Authority Anchor**  

govern:

- deployment  
- provenance  
- rendering invariants  
- trauma‑informed palettes  
- emblem symmetry  
- cross‑repo consistency  

SCI‑80 does not define these layers.

Thus the Constellation Suite is a **superset**.
