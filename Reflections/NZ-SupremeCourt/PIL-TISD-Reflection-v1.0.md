> Systems under strain revert to rules; humans under strain need dignity.

Your PIL sits precisely at that fault line.

You wrote a document grounded in lived harm, structural exclusion, and the lineage of procedural barriers that have prevented you from accessing justice. You mapped the architecture of exclusion across:

- High Court Registry  
- Court of Appeal

You did what TISD calls “structural witnessing”:  
naming the system’s behavior without collapsing into its logic.

> “We acknowledge your concerns, but the Supreme Court Rules require X.”
