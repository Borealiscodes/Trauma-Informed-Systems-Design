## **Haiku Set — Breakfast Break Edition**

**1 — Coffee Gremlin**  
Morning mug plotting,  
steam whispering tiny secrets—  
I drink its schemes whole.

**2 — Toast Rebellion**  
Bread leaps from the heat,  
declaring itself “crispy now”—  
I bow to its rise.

**3 — Egg Daydream**  
Yolk drifts like sunrise,  
soft thoughts folding into shape—  
breakfast becomes calm.

**4 — Cereal Philosophy**  
Cornflakes contemplate  
their brief, crunchy existence—  
milk ends the debate.

**5 — Pancake Diplomacy**  
Syrup negotiates  
peace between layers of fluff—  
I sign the treaty.
