# **🍵 Koan of the Haiku That Became a Philosophy**  
*A light exchange between a Novice and a Master, recorded for gentle amusement.*

**The Novice says:**  
“I asked the system to build some haikus after governance was almost done.  
It rebuilt the entire philosophy instead of giving me the path  
when I tried to note it.”

**The Master says:**  
“Calculus is thus,  
or calcuffuss is thus—  
recording the verse,  
what is the fuss?”

A wink toward the old tradition:  
when asked for a death poem,  
the master simply reframed the moment.
