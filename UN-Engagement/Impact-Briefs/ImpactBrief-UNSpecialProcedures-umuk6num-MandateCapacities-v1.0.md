I. Top‑Line Impact Summary

Filing the umuk6num update significantly escalates international oversight by documenting that Ireland’s International Protection Appeals Tribunal (IPAT) is attempting to adjudicate a torture‑related case under conditions that violate Article 14 UN CAT, General Comment No. 3, CRPD Articles 11, 13, 16(4), and the Istanbul Protocol.

This filing activates five distinct UN mandate clusters, each with unique powers:

- Housing  
- Human Rights Defenders  
- Health  
- Transitional Justice  
- Disability

Together, these mandates create a multi‑vector accountability matrix capable of addressing Ireland’s systemic failures across judicial access, medical neglect, homelessness risk, retaliation, and procedural exclusion.
