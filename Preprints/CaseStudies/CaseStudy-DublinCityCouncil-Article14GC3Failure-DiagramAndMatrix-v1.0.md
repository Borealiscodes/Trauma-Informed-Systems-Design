📑 GC3 COMPLIANCE MATRIX — Dublin City Council (v1.0)

Mapped against Article 14 CAT, GC3, CRPD, ECHR, Basic Principles

| Obligation | Source | Requirement | Dublin City Council Action | Compliance |
|----------------|------------|------------------|--------------------------------|-----------------|
| Provide “as full rehabilitation as possible” | CAT Art. 14 | Holistic rehabilitation including legal & social services | No rehabilitation; no coordination; no social‑legal support | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Establish effective & accessible bodies | GC3 ¶15 | Bodies must be accessible, effective, non‑fragmented | Fragmented routing; refusal to respond; “not our remit” | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Provide holistic rehabilitation | GC3 ¶11 | Medical, psychological, legal, and social services | Only housing silo; no legal/social integration | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Provide rehabilitation ASAP | GC3 ¶15 | Immediate access after assessment | Delayed >1 year; no assessment | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Access must not depend on judicial processes | GC3 ¶115 | Rehabilitation independent of courts | “Contact the Courts” response | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Victim-centered planning | GC3 ¶11 | Collaboration with survivor | No collaboration; no warm transfer | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Provide redress, not just allow seeking | GC3 ¶20 | Right to obtain redress | No redress provided | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Provide services regardless of where torture occurred | GC3 ¶23, ¶27 | Universal obligation | Refusal based on remit | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Safeguard against violence & abuse | CRPD Art. 16(4) | Protection from harm | No safeguarding; exposure to homelessness | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Ensure access to justice | CRPD Art. 13 | Accommodations & accessibility | No accommodations; refusal to engage | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Provide effective remedy | ECHR Art. 13 | Functional remedy pathway | No remedy; no pathway | ❌ Non‑compliant |
| Provide restitution, rehabilitation, guarantees of non‑repetition | Basic Principles §§18–21 | Full reparation | None provided | ❌ Non‑compliant |
