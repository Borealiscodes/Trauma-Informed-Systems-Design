## 🔹 4 — NDIE Encoding Rules  

### 🧩 Rule 1 — Non‑Dual Encoding  
Tiles encode **structure**, not story:

- no symbolic meaning  
- no mythic mapping  
- no character or plot  

### 🔗 Rule 2 — Holonomy‑Aware Binding  
Tiles must bind to loops:

- DenseTiles → Scan / Assembly  
- OrderedTiles → Assembly / Projection  
- CoherentTiles → Projection / Stability  
- FreeTiles → Escape / diffusion  

### 🛡️ Rule 3 — Anti‑Collapse  
- no forced promotion of DenseTiles → CoherentTiles  
- no skipping loops  
- no collapsing FreeTiles into narrative “freedom”
