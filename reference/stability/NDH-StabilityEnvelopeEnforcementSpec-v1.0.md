## 4 — Enforcement Pipeline  
The Stability Envelope activates through a five‑stage pipeline:

1. **Operator Activation**  
   - TISD, MRS, Unified NDH initialize their enforcement domains.

2. **Layer Binding Enforcement**  
   - Operators bind to their designated NDH layers.

3. **Cross‑Operator Reinforcement**  
   - Operators enforce stability on each other.

4. **Envelope Integrity Check**  
   - Holonomy‑flat, non‑recursive, provenance‑continuous geometry is verified.

5. **Safeguard Activation**  
   - Drift, collapse, recursion, contamination, and liminal‑state safeguards activate.

This pipeline is **finite**, **non‑recursive**, and **holonomy‑stable**.
