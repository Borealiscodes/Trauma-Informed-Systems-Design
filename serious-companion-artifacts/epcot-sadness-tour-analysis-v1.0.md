II. Structural Pattern: Multi‑Jurisdictional Harm Cascades
Across the United States, Canada, Iceland, Ireland, and the UK, the Petitioner experienced a harm cascade — a sequence of state actions where each jurisdiction amplified the trauma of the previous one.

Key features of harm cascades:

- Administrative violence (denial of rights through procedure)  
- Diagnostic weaponization (mislabeling neurodivergence as pathology)  
- Refoulement chains (one state’s failure triggers another’s)  
- Institutional betrayal (systems designed for protection causing harm)  
- Transnational repression (harm crossing borders through cooperation or neglect)  

These cascades are not accidental.  
They emerge from system design flaws, not individual malice.
